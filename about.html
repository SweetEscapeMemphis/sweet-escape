Locally owned in Memphis

A neighborhood shop built for your kind of sweet

Sweet Escape brings hand-dipped ice cream, self-serve frozen yogurt, gelato, sorbet, milkshakes, and playful specialty desserts together at one Whitten Road shop near Bartlett.

See today’s menuPlan a visit

What you’ll find

One stop, plenty of ways to build dessert

Choose a classic or adventurous hand-dipped scoop, swirl your own frozen yogurt, explore Italian-style gelato and fruit-forward sorbetto, or build a milkshake. Specialty options include warm cookie and brownie bases, cookie ice cream sandwiches, ice cream nachos, and other rotating creations.

Availability changes as flavors rotate. Our live menu is the official source for what the shop has published as available today.

Clear choices

Our catalog connects flavors with published nutrition, ingredient, and allergen information when it is available. Recipes can change, so guests with allergies should always ask the shop to verify the current product label.

Honest availability

Catalog pages document what Sweet Escape may carry; they do not promise that every item is in stock. The live menu separates the complete catalog from today’s selection.

Local welcome

Sweet Escape serves Memphis from 1674 Whitten Rd, Suite 106, close to Bartlett and the surrounding northeast Memphis community.

How this site is maintained

First-party information, explained plainly

This is Sweet Escape’s official website. Menu, location, hours, rewards, and shop updates are published by the business. Educational guides distinguish general dessert information from current shop availability and link readers back to the live menu.

Nutrition and allergen pages reproduce or summarize published product information and include cautions where formulas, labels, or shared equipment may matter. Website information is reviewed when products or store details change.

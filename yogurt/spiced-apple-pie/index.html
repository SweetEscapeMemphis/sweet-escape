An apple-and-spice frozen yogurt

Spiced Apple Pie is a Honey Hill Farms frozen yogurt listed in Sweet Escape’s seasonal catalog. The manufacturer describes an apple-and-spice flavor. The published ingredient statement below names cinnamon along with milk, cream, yogurt cultures, and natural and artificial flavors. This is a frozen yogurt flavor, rather than a slice of pie or an apple ice cream.

Compare fall dessert flavors

Choosing between apple and pumpkin? Compare this yogurt with Pumpkin Pie frozen yogurt, which has its own ingredient statement and nutrition label. Sweet Escape also catalogs Apple Crisp ice cream and Bourbon Vanilla Apple Crisp gelato. These are separate products, so use each item’s label and current menu listing.

Ingredients

Milk, Liquid Sugar (sugar, water), Corn Syrup, Nonfat Milk, Whey, Cream, Contains less than 2% of Mono-and Diglycerides, Cellulose Gum, Polysorbate 80, Guar Gum, Carrageenan, Natural and Artificial Flavors, Caramel Color, Citric Acid, Xanthan Gum, FD&C Yellow #6 and Cinnamon. Active Yogurt Cultures: S. Thermophilus, L. Bulgaricus, L. Acidophilus, B. Bifidus and L. Casei.

Allergen information

This product is manufactured in a facility that also processes peanuts, tree nuts, eggs, soy, milk, and wheat products.

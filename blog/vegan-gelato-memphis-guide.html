Reading the vegan label

How to Find Sweet Escape's Vegan Gelato Choices in Memphis

Published September 9, 2026 · Sweet Escape, Memphis

Looking for vegan gelato in Memphis starts with two separate questions: which products appear in Sweet Escape's catalog, and which choices are listed in stock right now? The website answers both, but on different pages. This guide shows where to find the manufacturer-labeled vegan entries, how to check the live menu, and why the current container label still matters before you order.

Start with the Vegan category in the gelato catalog

Open the gelato and sorbetto catalog and choose the Vegan category. The catalog currently documents two entries supplied under that label: Vegan Vanilla and Vegan Chocolate. Villa Dolce describes Vegan Vanilla as a non-dairy gelato featuring Madagascar vanilla bean. Its Vegan Chocolate listing is described as a non-dairy, dairy-free gelato made with dark chocolate.

Those are catalog entries, not a promise that both flavors are in the shop every day. The catalog is the place to learn how a product is labeled and read its published notes; current availability comes from the live menu.

Check the live menu before leaving home

Next, visit the live in-stock menu and select the Gelato tab. A current item opens its full catalog card, where you can see whether it carries the Gelato, Sorbetto, or Vegan label. The page also displays its latest update time. At publication, neither Vegan Vanilla nor Vegan Chocolate was listed in stock, so the catalog alone should not be used to plan a same-day visit around either flavor.

Availability can change after this article is published. Check the live page close to your visit instead of relying on this dated snapshot. The live-menu guide explains the search box, category tabs, update time, and product links in more detail.

A Vegan label is not a complete allergen answer

The catalog repeats the manufacturer's Vegan label, but it does not turn that category into a broad dietary or cross-contact guarantee. A complete major-allergen statement was not published online for Vegan Vanilla. For Vegan Chocolate, the catalog specifically notes that chocolate may introduce soy. Both cards tell visitors to ask staff to verify the current container label.

That distinction is important when an allergy or dietary need affects your choice. Product formulas, labels, and in-store handling can change. Use the online cards as a starting point, then ask the shop about the product in use before ordering. The nutrition and allergen guide explains why category filters and blank allergen fields should never be read as safety certifications.

Use a simple two-page check

  1. Open the gelato catalog and choose Vegan to review the documented entries.
  2. Open the live menu and select Gelato to see what is currently listed.
  3. Read the matching product card and its cautions.
  4. Ask the shop to verify the current label when dietary details matter.

This sequence keeps product information and availability in their proper places. It also avoids assuming that words such as vegan, non-dairy, or dairy-free answer every allergen or cross-contact question.

Plan your Memphis visit

Sweet Escape is at 1674 Whitten Rd, Suite 106, Memphis, TN 38134, near Bartlett. Regular hours are Monday–Thursday 12–9 PM and Friday–Sunday 12–10 PM. The Visit page includes the shop phone number, map, and directions if you want to confirm product details before traveling.

Beyond the scoop

Two Cookie Desserts to Explore at Sweet Escape

Published September 5, 2026 · Sweet Escape, Memphis

Sometimes the decision is not simply which ice cream flavor sounds good. Sweet Escape's specialties menu lists two cookie-based ways to pair a scoop with chocolate-chip cookies: the Cookie Dough Base and Cookie Ice Cream Sandwiches. Here is what the website says about each one, how to compare the formats, and what to verify before a Memphis visit.

Cookie Dough Base starts warm and open-faced

The specialties page describes the Cookie Dough Base as freshly baked chocolate-chip cookie dough with one ice cream scoop on top. It is the open-faced choice of the pair: a warm cookie-dough base underneath the scoop rather than cookies surrounding it. The menu says the ice cream flavor is chosen in the shop, but it does not publish a price or promise that a particular flavor or specialty will be available at every visit.

Cookie Ice Cream Sandwiches put the scoop in the middle

The Cookie Ice Cream Sandwich listing describes a chosen ice cream flavor pressed between two chocolate-chip cookies. That makes its structure easy to distinguish from the Cookie Dough Base: two cookies around the ice cream instead of one warm dough base below it. The page does not state a fixed flavor, so the current scoop lineup is the useful place to begin comparing the ice cream part of the dessert.

Use the live menu for current scoop choices

Open the live in-stock menu and choose the Scoops tab to see the hand-dipped flavors currently listed. The page includes an update time and links each result to its catalog entry. Availability can change, so check close to the visit and keep another scoop choice in mind. The live-menu guide explains the search box, category tabs, and product links.

The stock page tracks scoop, yogurt, and gelato flavors; it does not show daily availability for the Cookie Dough Base or Cookie Ice Cream Sandwiches. If one of those specialties is the reason for the trip, call the shop for current confirmation instead of assuming that a website menu listing is an availability guarantee.

Separate scoop details from cookie-component details

Sweet Escape's scoop catalog provides published nutrition and allergen information for individual hand-dipped flavors. Those product cards cover the scoop itself; they do not provide a complete ingredient, nutrition, or allergen statement for the cookie-dough base, sandwich cookies, toppings, or the finished specialty.

When ingredients or allergens matter, review the selected scoop's card and ask the shop to verify the current labels for every other component. Recipes and handling can change, and neither dessert name guarantees dietary suitability or freedom from cross-contact.

Plan the Memphis stop

Sweet Escape is at 1674 Whitten Rd, Suite 106, Memphis, TN 38134, near Bartlett. Regular hours are Monday–Thursday 12–9 PM and Friday–Sunday 12–10 PM. The Visit page includes the phone number, map, and directions, making it the right place to confirm the practical details after comparing the two cookie desserts.

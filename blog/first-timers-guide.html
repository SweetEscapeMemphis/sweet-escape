What to Order at Sweet Escape: A First-Timer's Guide

Published August 28, 2026 · Updated August 31, 2026 · Sweet Escape, Memphis

A first visit to Sweet Escape starts with a useful choice: hand-dipped ice cream, self-serve frozen yogurt with toppings, gelato or sorbetto. The shop also lists milkshakes and cookie-based loaded treats. This guide helps you compare those categories without relying on a flavor that may have rotated out before you arrive.

Start with the live menu

The current in-stock menu is the quickest way to see what the Memphis shop is listing today. It separates scoop flavors, yogurt, and gelato so you can narrow the decision before leaving home. The larger flavor pages are catalogs; they are helpful for learning about the range, but a catalog entry is not a promise that a flavor is in the case on a particular day.

Use the catalogs to narrow the choices

The catalog pages do more than list names. The scoop page has search, category, and declared-allergen filters. The yogurt and gelato pages also provide search and product-detail tools for their own collections. That makes it easier to build a short list around a flavor family or a published label detail before checking what is currently in stock.

Treat the process as two steps: use a catalog to understand the options, then use the live menu to confirm the current lineup. If one flavor is the reason for the trip, keep a second choice in mind because the stock list can change. A backup can stay in the same category or come from another part of the menu; the goal is simply to avoid treating the full catalog like a daily availability promise. Bookmark the live page if you want to check it again on the day of the visit.

Four easy places to begin

  • Hand-dipped ice cream: browse the scoop flavor catalog, then confirm the day's selection on the live menu.
  • Self-serve frozen yogurt: use the yogurt catalog to compare flavors and published product details, then choose your own toppings in the shop.
  • Gelato or sorbetto: the gelato catalog brings the available nutrition, ingredient, and allergen information into one searchable page.
  • Another kind of treat: milkshakes and cookie-based loaded treats are also among the products Sweet Escape lists.

Check product details before choosing

If ingredients or allergens matter to you, review the published nutrition and allergen information before your visit. The scoop, yogurt, and gelato pages distinguish declared allergens from additional notes and explain where information is limited. Product formulas and in-store handling can change, so use those pages as a starting point and contact the shop when you need current details.

Plan the visit

Sweet Escape is at 1674 Whitten Rd, Suite 106 in Memphis, near Bartlett. Regular hours are Monday–Thursday 12–9 PM and Friday–Sunday 12–10 PM. The Visit page has the phone number, map, directions, and the same hours in one place.

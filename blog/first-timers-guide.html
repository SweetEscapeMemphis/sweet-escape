What to Order at Sweet Escape: A First-Timer's Guide

Published August 28, 2026 · Sweet Escape, Memphis

Walking into Sweet Escape for the first time, you'll see three stations: hand-dipped scoop ice cream behind the counter, a self-serve frozen yogurt wall, and a gelato case. Everything works on one simple idea — you pay by weight, so you build exactly the treat you want and only pay for what lands in your cup.

How pay-by-weight works

Grab a cup, fill it with whatever combination you like — froyo, toppings, a scoop on top, sauce — and we weigh it at the register. There's no "small, medium, large" decision to get wrong, and mixing categories is encouraged. Not sure about a flavor? Ask for a sample first. Our team hands them out happily; it's the number one tip returning customers mention in their Google reviews.

Three easy first orders

  • The classic: a hand-dipped scoop — cookie dough and strawberry cheesecake are perennial favorites — in a cup or cone. Check the live in-stock menu before you come; the lineup rotates.
  • The builder: self-serve frozen yogurt with your own topping combination. Start light — the cup fills up faster than you think.
  • The loaded treat: warm cookie base with a scoop on top, or a thick milkshake if you're in a drinking-your-dessert mood.

Watching sugar or dairy?

The rotating case usually includes sorbet and no-sugar-added options, and our nutrition and allergen notes cover every scoop, yogurt, and gelato flavor so you can check ingredients before you order.

When to come

We're at 1674 Whitten Rd, Suite 106 in Memphis — in the Goodlett Commons shopping center, minutes from Bartlett. Open Monday–Thursday 12–9 PM and Friday–Sunday 12–10 PM, which makes us a solid late-evening dessert stop after dinner nearby.

Pumpkin Pie Frozen Yogurt: A Little Fall at Sweet Escape

Published September 27, 2026 · Sweet Escape, Memphis

Some dessert decisions start with chocolate. Others start with a very particular craving: pumpkin, pie spices, and a reason to put the usual order on hold. If that sounds like your kind of afternoon, meet Pumpkin Pie frozen yogurt at Sweet Escape in Memphis. This seasonal flavor brings real pumpkin puree, ground cinnamon, and ground nutmeg into a frozen-yogurt recipe inspired by the familiar pie.

The appeal is simple: you can choose a dessert with a pumpkin-pie theme without ordering a slice of pie. It belongs to our self-serve frozen-yogurt selection, so it is also a different direction from our hand-dipped ice cream and gelato. Here is a closer look at what makes this flavor worth considering, how it compares with our other bakery-inspired yogurts, and how to make it the focus of your next cup.

What puts the pumpkin in Pumpkin Pie?

Start with the ingredient that gives the flavor its name. Pumpkin puree appears in the Pumpkin Pie Base used in this yogurt. That base also includes sweetened condensed skim milk, ground cinnamon, and ground nutmeg. The product description highlights pumpkin puree and nutmeg, while the ingredient list gives cinnamon its own place in the picture. This is not simply a flavor with an autumn-themed name and no pumpkin behind it.

The larger recipe includes milk, nonfat milk, cream, whey, liquid sugar, corn syrup, and active yogurt cultures. Together, those details explain the kind of dessert you are choosing: a dairy-based frozen yogurt with a pumpkin-and-spice direction. They do not make it a pie, a pumpkin puree dessert, or a dairy-free alternative. If you are deciding between a familiar vanilla order and something more specifically seasonal, that distinction is a useful starting point.

You can find the full ingredient list and label resources on our Pumpkin Pie frozen yogurt page. The recipe also lists natural flavors, colors, and stabilizers; the short flavor description is not a substitute for those complete details. For a quick flavor decision, though, remember three names: pumpkin, cinnamon, and nutmeg.

Pie inspiration, frozen-yogurt format

Think of “Pumpkin Pie” as the flavor inspiration, not a promise of a crust tucked into every spoonful. The ingredient list does not identify pie-crust pieces. That matters if the part of pie you love most is the pastry: this yogurt takes the pumpkin-and-spice route instead. It gives you a way to explore that flavor family in a different dessert format, with the frozen yogurt itself as the starting point.

There is a useful question to ask before choosing it: are you looking for pumpkin specifically, or simply for something that reminds you of a bakery dessert? If pumpkin is the answer, this flavor has a clear reason to be on your shortlist. If cinnamon or an apple-pie theme is the real draw, two neighboring choices in our yogurt catalog deserve a look. The names may share a seasonal mood, but they are not interchangeable recipes.

Pumpkin Pie, Cinnamon Bun, or Spiced Apple Pie?

Ooey Gooey Cinnamon Bun takes its cue from cinnamon buns and cream-cheese icing. Its ingredients include cinnamon and a cake base containing butter, cream cheese, and egg yolks. If the icing idea is what catches your attention, that is a meaningful difference from Pumpkin Pie. The word “warm” in its flavor description describes the cinnamon-bun theme; you are still choosing frozen yogurt.

Spiced Apple Pie follows a baked-apple-and-pie-spice flavor direction. Cinnamon appears in its ingredients, but the published list does not identify apple pieces or apple puree. Choose it for the stated apple-pie flavor, not an expectation of fruit chunks. Pumpkin Pie, by contrast, explicitly lists pumpkin puree. Reading those details turns three tempting names into three clearer choices.

Here is the simplest way to decide: choose Pumpkin Pie when pumpkin is the point, Cinnamon Bun when the cinnamon-and-icing idea sounds right, and Spiced Apple Pie when an apple-pie flavor is what you had in mind. This is a comparison of their descriptions and ingredients, not a ranking of sweetness or spice intensity. Those details do not tell us which will taste strongest to you, and personal preference is part of the fun.

Give the pumpkin flavor room in your cup

Our self-serve frozen yogurt comes with the option to choose toppings. For a first Pumpkin Pie cup, consider starting with the yogurt on its own. That is an easy way to decide whether the pumpkin-and-spice theme is what you wanted before introducing another flavor. A simple cup is a complete choice; you do not need a long list of additions to make it feel intentional.

If you do want toppings, make one decision first: are you trying to keep the focus on pumpkin, or create a more mixed dessert? Let that answer guide your choices from what is available in the shop. You might prefer a single addition rather than several competing ones. There is no required topping combination here, and we are not promising a particular topping will be available when you arrive.

For someone who usually builds a busy cup, this is a chance to reverse the process. Pick the main flavor first and let everything else be optional. For someone who always orders the same yogurt, it is a straightforward way to try a different flavor direction without also changing every other part of the order. Our frozen-yogurt cup guide has more help with that decision.

A different choice from your usual chocolate order

Not sure whether to leave your usual favorite behind? Think about what you want from this particular dessert stop. If you arrived wanting chocolate, a seasonal name alone may not change that. But if you are curious about a pie-inspired flavor and have been looking for a reason to try one, Pumpkin Pie offers a specific starting point. You are choosing a pumpkin recipe with two named spices, rather than simply choosing whatever sounds newest.

After trying it, keep your own comparison simple. Was the pumpkin theme the part you enjoyed, or did the cinnamon-and-nutmeg idea interest you more? Would you order it on its own again, or take a different direction next time? Those answers are more useful than trying to find one flavor everyone should prefer. They can also help you decide whether a future Cinnamon Bun order belongs on your list. A new favorite is personal; it does not need to replace every old one.

Ingredients and allergens: the important details

Pumpkin Pie frozen yogurt contains milk. Its ingredient list includes several dairy ingredients, so it is not a dairy-free choice. The product information also carries this facility notice: “This product is manufactured in a facility that also processes peanuts, tree nuts, eggs, soy, milk, and wheat products.” That notice remains relevant even when a particular allergen is not marked as present in the product’s allergen table.

Do not treat an allergen-table “No” as a guarantee against cross-contact. Product formulas and in-store handling can change, and toppings introduce their own ingredients and handling considerations. If an allergy or dietary restriction affects your order, review the complete product information and contact the shop about current details before choosing. The goal is an informed decision, not an assumption based on a flavor name.

The Pumpkin Pie page also links to its nutrition information. Use the serving amount on that label when reading the numbers; it does not automatically describe the cup you build or any toppings you add. Pumpkin in the recipe is a flavor detail, not a reason to attach health claims to the dessert. This is a treat to choose for the flavor you want.

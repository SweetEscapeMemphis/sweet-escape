How to Build Your Frozen Yogurt Cup at Sweet Escape

Published September 3, 2026 · Sweet Escape, Memphis

A self-serve frozen yogurt cup lets you begin with a flavor and finish with toppings that make it your own. At Sweet Escape in Memphis, the useful first step happens before you reach the shop: check the current yogurt lineup, compare the published product details, and arrive with a flexible plan. This guide keeps that process simple without assuming which flavors or toppings will be available during a particular visit.

Check the frozen yogurt listed today

Start on the live in-stock menu. Select the Yogurt tab to focus on the frozen yogurt and sorbet choices currently marked in stock. The page also shows when the availability list was last updated. Because the lineup can rotate, check it close to your visit and keep a second current flavor in mind. The live-menu guide explains every tool on that page if you want a closer look.

Use the yogurt catalog to compare flavors

After you find a current option, open the frozen yogurt catalog to read its full listing. The catalog can be searched by flavor name or description, and its category controls organize entries such as nonfat, yogurt, no-sugar-added, and sorbet. Those labels help you browse; they do not make a general health or dietary promise.

Each flavor card brings together the available description, nutrition panel, ingredients, declared allergens, and facility note. The catalog is broader than the live lineup, so use it for product information and return to the in-stock page for current availability.

Choose the flavor before the finishing touches

Think about what you want the frozen yogurt to contribute to the finished cup. A familiar flavor can be a straightforward base, while a more distinctive current choice can stay at the center of the combination. You do not need a complicated plan: pick the current flavor that sounds good, then decide whether the toppings should complement it or add a contrast in flavor or texture.

Add toppings from the in-store selection

Sweet Escape offers toppings for its self-serve frozen yogurt, but the website does not publish a guaranteed daily topping list. Browse what is available when you arrive instead of planning around one specific item. A simple approach is to add one finishing idea at a time, look at how the cup is coming together, and stop when the combination feels complete. That keeps the flavor you chose easy to recognize.

Pause for ingredient and allergen details

If ingredients or allergens matter to you, review the individual yogurt listing and its facility note before choosing. The catalog's declared allergen filter can help locate entries, but a filter result is not a guarantee of dietary suitability or freedom from cross-contact. Topping details are not published on the site. Call the shop when you need current information that the online listing does not answer.

Plan your Memphis frozen yogurt stop

Sweet Escape is at 1674 Whitten Rd, Suite 106, Memphis, TN 38134, near Bartlett. Regular hours are Monday–Thursday 12–9 PM and Friday–Sunday 12–10 PM. The Visit page includes the phone number, map, and turn-by-turn directions. Check the yogurt lineup, choose a flexible flavor plan, and finish the cup with the toppings you find in the shop.

Sweet Escape vs. Venchi: A Gelato Guide

Published September 26, 2026 · Sweet Escape, Memphis

Sweet Escape and Venchi both put gelato on the menu, but they build different dessert experiences around it. Venchi describes its stores as “Chocogelaterie,” connecting an Italian chocolate heritage with gelato and chocolate-based treats. Sweet Escape serves gelato and sorbetto as part of a wider Memphis menu that also includes hand-dipped ice cream, self-serve frozen yogurt, milkshakes, and specialty desserts. Here is a factual way to compare the formats before deciding what fits your plans.

Venchi connects chocolate and gelato

Venchi’s official history traces the brand to 1878, when Silviano Venchi began experimenting with chocolate in a small Turin workshop. The company says its stores later added fresh Italian gelato alongside chocolates, forming the Chocogelateria concept. It presents chocolate in several forms, from bars and pralines to gelato and gelatoshakes.

The official Venchi gelato page highlights best-selling flavors and suggested combinations. Its published list includes chocolate-led flavors, pistachio and hazelnut choices, fruity options, and vegan-labeled mango, strawberry, and extra-dark chocolate selections. The page also describes customizable gelatoshakes and optional spreads or crunchy toppings.

Those are brand-level descriptions, not a guarantee that every Venchi store has every flavor or format. A visitor should use Venchi’s store finder and confirm the current menu for the location under consideration.

Sweet Escape makes gelato one of several paths

Sweet Escape’s gelato and sorbetto catalog documents the broader collection and the product details available for each entry. For a same-day decision, use the live in-stock menu; the catalog is intentionally larger than the rotating selection.

Gelato is only one route through the menu. Visitors can compare the hand-dipped scoop catalog, browse self-serve frozen yogurt choices, or review milkshakes and loaded cookie-based desserts on the specialties page. The complete item directory brings every verified catalog entry together for anyone planning across categories.

Labels such as “vegan,” “sorbet,” or “gelato” do not answer every ingredient, allergen, or cross-contact question. Review the published product details and facility cautions, then ask the relevant shop to confirm its current product label and handling conditions. This comparison does not establish personal dietary safety.

Which menu structure fits your visit?

Venchi may fit someone specifically researching a chocolate-centered gelato shop, chocolate-inspired flavors, or the brand’s gelatoshake and topping combinations. Sweet Escape may fit a Memphis or Bartlett group that wants to compare gelato with hand-dipped ice cream, self-serve frozen yogurt, milkshakes, and specialty desserts in one place. That is a difference in documented focus and variety—not a claim that one brand is universally better.

Sweet Escape is at 1674 Whitten Rd, Suite 106, Memphis, TN 38134, near Bartlett. Posted hours are Monday–Thursday noon–9 PM and Friday–Sunday noon–10 PM.

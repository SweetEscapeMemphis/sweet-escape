Four Gelato and Sorbetto Flavors on the Live Menu

Published September 6, 2026 · Sweet Escape, Memphis

Sweet Escape's live in-stock menu currently lists four choices in its gelato section: three gelatos and one sorbetto. That makes the lineup compact enough to compare before a Memphis or Bartlett dessert stop. Here is what the published catalog says about Alphonso Mango, Torta Al Limone, Birthday Cake, and Traditional Cookies & Cream—and which label details deserve a closer look. The comparison also shows why choices in one menu section can require different label checks.

Alphonso Mango is the sorbetto choice

Alphonso Mango is the one current flavor categorized as Sorbetto. The catalog describes fragrant Alphonso mango from India in a bright, velvety sorbetto. If you are new to the term, our sorbetto guide explains how Sweet Escape organizes it in the gelato catalog.

The online entry does not include a complete major-allergen statement or numeric nutrition panel. “Sorbetto” should not be used as an allergen, dairy, vegan, or dietary guarantee; ask the shop to verify the current container label before ordering when those details matter.

Torta Al Limone brings lemon-pie details

Torta Al Limone (Lemon Pie) is described as creamy lemon-pie gelato with bright citrus ribbons and delicate shortbread pieces. Its published allergen details list milk, egg, wheat, and soy, with peanuts and tree nuts in the “may contain” note. The catalog does not show a complete numeric nutrition panel for this flavor.

Birthday Cake leans into cake and sprinkles

The Birthday Cake gelato entry describes vanilla cake gelato with colorful sprinkles and cake pieces. Milk is the allergen listed on the card, while its caution says the cake and sprinkle components may introduce egg, wheat, or soy. That extra note is important: the short summary is only a starting point, not the entire label story.

Traditional Cookies & Cream has the fullest online panel

Traditional Cookies & Cream combines sweet-cream gelato with chocolate sandwich-cookie pieces. The published panel uses a 1/2-cup (88g) serving and lists 220 calories, 11g fat, 25g carbohydrates, 21g sugars, 4g protein, and 100mg sodium. Its declared allergens are milk, wheat, and soy; the card also says it may contain peanuts, egg, and tree nuts.

Check the live list and the label separately

The live menu answers “what is listed now,” while the gelato catalog answers “what details are published for this flavor.” Check both close to your visit, because availability changes. Product formulas and handling can change, too. Our nutrition and allergen guide explains how to use those fields without treating incomplete online data as proof of dietary suitability or freedom from cross-contact.

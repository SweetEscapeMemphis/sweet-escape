A guide to three lemon-themed catalog entries

Lemon Pie Gelato or Lemon Sorbetto? Three Lemon Desserts at Sweet Escape

Published October 4, 2026 · Sweet Escape, Memphis

When you are in the mood for lemon, a menu can offer more than one way to follow that bright citrus idea. Sweet Escape’s published gelato and sorbetto catalog includes Torta Al Limone (Lemon Pie), Zesty Lemon, and Limoncello. All three share a lemon theme, but their categories and descriptions are not interchangeable. One is described as lemon-pie gelato with shortbread pieces; the other two are sorbettos with their own distinct wording.

This guide helps Memphis-area guests compare those three records without inventing a recipe, promising a flavor is in the case today, or drawing dietary conclusions from a product name. If you are choosing what to explore, begin with the description that sounds closest to the kind of dessert you have in mind. Then check the product page, review the label guidance, and consult the live menu before making a trip around a particular item.

Start with the three names—and their categories

Torta Al Limone (Lemon Pie) is categorized as gelato. Its published description says “creamy lemon-pie gelato with bright citrus ribbons and delicate shortbread pieces.” That sentence gives a little more structure than a single flavor word: it identifies a pie-inspired gelato, citrus ribbons, and shortbread pieces. It does not list every ingredient, quantify any component, or provide a full recipe.

Zesty Lemon is categorized as sorbetto. The record describes it as “a clean, bright lemon sorbetto with a crisp citrus finish.” The emphasis is on lemon and its crisp citrus finish, rather than pie components. The description does not claim a particular recipe or disclose a full ingredient list.

Limoncello is also categorized as sorbetto. Its description reads: “Luminous lemon sorbetto inspired by the bright flavor of Italian limoncello.” The key phrase is “inspired by the bright flavor.” That identifies the idea behind the description; it does not establish ingredients or whether alcohol is present or absent. If that detail matters to you, ask the shop to check the current container label.

If you want the pie-inspired description

Torta Al Limone is the one to open first if the shortbread detail caught your attention. The public product wording puts lemon-pie gelato, citrus ribbons, and delicate shortbread pieces together in a single description. That makes the listing more specific than a generic lemon label and gives you a concrete picture of the components the product record names.

There is a practical difference between a flavor description and an ingredient statement. “Lemon pie” communicates the style of the item; the named ribbons and shortbread pieces add further detail. But the product description is not a complete recipe, and it should not be stretched to answer questions it was not written to answer. It does not identify every ingredient, the source of every component, or the amounts involved. If you need those details, use the current container label and ask the shop rather than treating a short description as exhaustive.

The published Torta Al Limone catalog record declares milk, egg, wheat, and soy. It also includes a may-contain statement for peanuts and tree nuts. Keep both parts of that published note together when reviewing the page. An allergy decision should be based on current product information and the shop’s verification—not on the word “lemon,” “pie,” or the appearance of a flavor.

If you want the most direct lemon wording

Zesty Lemon is described in concise citrus terms: clean, bright lemon with a crisp citrus finish. That can make it an easy catalog entry to compare with the more elaborate pie-inspired Torta Al Limone description. It is listed as sorbetto, and the wording stays focused on lemon rather than naming pastry or cookie components.

Concise wording is useful when you are scanning a catalog, but it is not a complete product specification. The Zesty Lemon record does not publish a full ingredient list or a complete major-allergen statement online. It specifically tells guests to ask staff to verify the current container label. So while the listing describes the item as sorbetto, that alone should not be used to claim the product meets a particular dietary need.

If the Italian-inspired name caught your eye

Limoncello’s catalog description refers to Italian limoncello as its flavor inspiration. That phrase creates a clear distinction from Zesty Lemon’s direct lemon wording and Torta Al Limone’s pie-inspired wording, while keeping both Limoncello and Zesty Lemon in the sorbetto category.

It is also where care with language matters most. A product name or inspiration does not tell you whether alcohol is part of the current product. The online description does not make an alcohol-content statement in either direction. If you need a definite answer—for personal, religious, health, or family reasons—ask Sweet Escape to verify the current container label before ordering. Do not treat the name as proof of alcohol content or as proof of its absence.

The Limoncello entry also does not publish a complete major-allergen statement online. The note asks guests to check with staff against the current container label. That is important even when the product’s category sounds simple: a category is not an ingredient list, and a flavor description is not a dietary certification.

A simple way to choose among the three

If you are browsing for a dessert idea rather than resolving an ingredient question, this quick sequence can help:

  1. Choose the description that matches your curiosity. Shortbread and citrus ribbons point you to Torta Al Limone’s pie-inspired wording. Direct lemon language points to Zesty Lemon. An Italian limoncello flavor inspiration points to Limoncello.
  2. Check the category. Torta Al Limone is listed as gelato; Zesty Lemon and Limoncello are listed as sorbetto.
  3. Read the item page. Each page provides the entry’s description and the caution attached to that specific product record.
  4. Check what is in the shop today. Catalog pages describe items but do not guarantee that a particular flavor is currently available. Use the live menu or call if your visit depends on one option.

Allergy, ingredient, and nutrition questions deserve their own check

The three catalog records do not publish the same kinds of details. Torta Al Limone lists allergens and a may-contain caution. Zesty Lemon and Limoncello do not have complete major-allergen statements published online, and both entries direct guests to verify the current container label. A missing online statement is not the same as a statement that a product contains none of the major allergens.

Similarly, a short description should not be treated as a complete list of ingredients or as a nutrition panel. The records reviewed for this guide do not provide a complete numeric nutrition label for these entries. If you have a specific ingredient, nutrition, or allergy question, read the current packaging information and ask the team to confirm what applies to the product currently being served. Do not infer dietary suitability or cross-contact safety from the product name, category, or an online description.

Questions guests commonly ask

Are Torta Al Limone and the lemon sorbettos the same flavor?

No. The records describe three distinct catalog entries: pie-inspired Torta Al Limone gelato, Zesty Lemon sorbetto, and Limoncello sorbetto inspired by the bright flavor of Italian limoncello.

Does “sorbetto” mean Zesty Lemon or Limoncello is safe for a particular diet?

No. The online product records do not provide complete major-allergen statements for those two entries. Ask staff to verify the current container label; do not infer dietary suitability from a category name.

Does the Limoncello name tell me whether the product contains alcohol?

No. The published description states that its lemon sorbetto is inspired by the bright flavor of Italian limoncello, but does not say whether alcohol is present or absent. Ask the shop to verify the current container label if the answer matters to you.

Can I count on finding these options today?

No catalog entry guarantees current availability. Check the live menu before visiting, and call if you need to confirm one particular flavor.

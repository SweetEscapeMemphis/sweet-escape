What Changed on Sweet Escape's September 19 Live Menu

Published September 20, 2026 · Sweet Escape, Memphis

Sweet Escape's live menu received a new stock update on September 19, 2026. The update keeps 20 hand-dipped scoop flavors, expands the frozen yogurt and sorbet list from eight choices to twelve, and changes one of the four gelato and sorbetto entries. This article records what changed between the September 8 and September 19 stock files.

Twelve scoop flavors joined the live list

The updated in-stock menu adds Amaretto Cherry, Banana Pudding, Cookie Dough, Lemon Cheesecake Bar, Peanut Butter Bullseye, Peanut Butter Cup, Sea Turtle, Strawberry Cheesecake, and Superman. Oregon Strawberry, Peaches & Cream, and Udderly Chocolate also appear in the current group but were already present in the prior snapshot.

The scoop list remains at 20 because twelve earlier entries rotated off as twelve others joined. Continuing choices include Blueberry Burst, Chocolate, Cool Mint Cookie, Cow Tippin', Pistachio Almond, Puppy Chow, Rush Hour, Vanilla Bean, Oregon Strawberry, Peaches & Cream, and Udderly Chocolate. Use the scoop catalog to open product details rather than relying only on a name.

The frozen yogurt list expanded to twelve

The September 19 update adds Birthday Cake, Cookies N' Cream, French Vanilla, Spiced Apple Pie, and Valencia Orange Sorbet to the live yogurt group. Cheesecake no longer appears in the new snapshot. Tahitian Vanilla NSA, Cookie Monster, Strawberry Sensations, Triple Chocolate, White Chocolate Mousse, Pink Lemonade Sorbet, and Watermelon Sorbet remain listed.

That means the live frozen-yogurt and sorbet group grew from eight entries to twelve. Browse the frozen yogurt catalog for published product information, then return to the stock page when availability is the deciding question.

One gelato entry changed

The four-item gelato group now lists Birthday Cake, Mint Chocolate Chip, NY Strawberry Cheesecake, and Roasted Banana Dulce de Leche. Birthday Cake replaces Alphonso Mango from the September 8 snapshot; the other three remain on the live list. The gelato and sorbetto catalog provides the broader collection and individual cards.

Treat this as a dated snapshot

This comparison is accurate to the two repository updates, but stock can change after publication. Check the live page close to your trip and note the update time displayed there. A catalog entry documents a product; it does not promise that the flavor is currently available. Refresh the live page before leaving home so its displayed update time reflects the latest published stock file.

Names and category labels are also not dietary or allergen guarantees. Read each product card separately, keep every published caution, and ask the shop to verify the current container label when an allergy or dietary need matters. The nutrition and allergen guide explains those fields in more detail.

Sorbetto explained

What Is Sorbetto? How to Find It at Sweet Escape

Published September 4, 2026 · Sweet Escape, Memphis

Sorbetto appears beside gelato on the Sweet Escape menu, but the unfamiliar word can make a simple dessert decision feel like a vocabulary quiz. The short answer is friendly: sorbetto is the Italian name for sorbet. The more useful answer is knowing where Sweet Escape lists it, how to check whether a sorbetto flavor is currently available, and where to read the product cautions before visiting the Memphis shop.

Sorbetto is the Italian name for sorbet

Villa Dolce, the manufacturer identified in Sweet Escape's gelato catalog, explains that sorbet and sorbetto refer to the same frozen dessert, with sorbetto being the Italian name. That is why the word appears in the same section as gelato while still having its own category on the Sweet Escape website.

Find the Sorbetto category in the gelato catalog

Open Sweet Escape's gelato and sorbetto catalog, then choose the Sorbetto category button. That filter narrows the broader catalog to entries the manufacturer labels as sorbetto. You can also search by a flavor name or description. Each card includes the available flavor description, allergen guidance, source links, and nutrition information when a public numeric label is available.

The catalog and live menu answer different questions

The catalog shows the larger collection Sweet Escape has documented. It does not mean every listed flavor is in the case at once. For current availability, use the live in-stock menu. Sorbetto is grouped under the Gelato tab on that page; opening a result takes you to its full catalog card, where the specific Gelato, Sorbetto, or Vegan label appears.

Check the availability timestamp, and keep another current option in mind if one flavor is the reason for your trip. The live-menu guide walks through the search box, category tabs, and product-card links.

Do not use the name as an allergen guarantee

Villa Dolce describes its sorbetto line as non-dairy, but Sweet Escape's catalog also notes that complete major-allergen statements are not published online for every sorbetto entry. Recipes, labels, and in-store handling can change. Review the individual card and ask the shop to verify the current container label when an allergy or dietary need matters. The word sorbetto by itself is not a promise about suitability or cross-contact.

How sorbetto fits into a Sweet Escape visit

If you are deciding among several frozen desserts, the gelato, ice cream, and frozen yogurt comparison explains how the website organizes each menu. Start with the category that sounds appealing, use the live page to confirm the current lineup, and open individual product cards when details matter. That sequence is more reliable than making assumptions from a product name.

Gelato vs. Ice Cream vs. Frozen Yogurt: What's the Difference?

Published August 28, 2026 · Updated August 31, 2026 · Sweet Escape, Memphis

Ice cream, frozen yogurt, gelato, and sorbetto appear in separate sections of the Sweet Escape menu. That organization matters: each section has its own catalog and product details, while the live menu shows the flavors the Memphis shop is currently listing. Here is the practical way to compare them before a visit.

Hand-dipped ice cream

The scoop flavor catalog covers the hand-dipped ice cream selection along with related sherbet, sorbet, yogurt, and no-sugar-added catalog entries. Each card brings the available nutrition facts, ingredients, and declared allergen information close to the flavor description. Use the catalog to compare choices, then check the in-stock menu for the day's lineup.

Gelato and sorbetto

Sweet Escape maintains a separate gelato and sorbetto catalog. It includes searchable product information from the listed supplier, with cautions where details are incomplete or labels may change. A flavor name alone is not enough to establish whether an item fits a dietary need, so review its current label information rather than relying on a general assumption about gelato or sorbetto.

Self-serve frozen yogurt

The frozen yogurt catalog is the place to compare the self-serve flavors and published product details. Frozen yogurt is also the category paired with the shop's toppings selection. As with the other catalogs, availability rotates; the separate live menu is the better source for what is being listed today.

What the product details can tell you

A category name is useful for navigation, but it does not answer every ingredient or dietary question. Recipes vary by flavor and supplier. The Sweet Escape catalogs therefore place the available nutrition facts, ingredients, and allergen notes on individual product cards instead of applying one broad claim to an entire category.

Read the wording closely. A declared allergen is different from an additional caution, and some gelato entries specifically direct visitors to verify the current container label. Terms such as sorbetto, vegan, or no-sugar-added should be read in the context of the individual listing; they are not a blanket guarantee about dairy, other ingredients, health effects, or in-store cross-contact. When the online information does not resolve a concern, contact the shop before choosing. The catalog and live stock list answer different questions, so use both when a detail matters.

How to choose before you arrive

  • Want a hand-dipped choice? Start with the scoop catalog.
  • Want self-serve yogurt and toppings? Review the yogurt catalog.
  • Want gelato or sorbetto? Search the gelato catalog.
  • Need current availability? Use the live in-stock menu.
  • Need ingredient or allergen details? Read the product notes and contact the shop when you need current confirmation.

The categories make it easier to browse, but the flavor and label details are what matter for a specific choice. Start online, then use the Visit page for the Whitten Road address, regular hours, phone number, and directions.

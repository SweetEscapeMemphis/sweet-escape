Gelato vs. Ice Cream vs. Frozen Yogurt: What's the Difference?

Published August 28, 2026 · Sweet Escape, Memphis

We serve all three under one roof at our Whitten Road shop, so we hear this question just about every day. They look similar in the case, but the three desserts are made differently, taste different, and suit different moods. Here's the honest breakdown.

Ice cream: the rich classic

Ice cream is churned from cream and milk with plenty of air whipped in, which gives it that fluffy, scoopable body. It's the richest of the three, and it carries chunky mix-ins — cookie dough, cheesecake pieces, candy — better than anything else. If you want a cone that tastes like childhood, this is the one. Browse our current scoop flavors to see the lineup.

Gelato: dense and intense

Gelato uses more milk and less cream than ice cream and is churned slowly, so far less air gets in. The result is denser, silkier, and served slightly warmer — which is why flavors hit harder. A small cup of gelato tastes more intense than a big scoop of ice cream. Our gelato and sorbetto case rotates, and the sorbetto side is dairy-free by nature.

Frozen yogurt: light and build-your-own

Froyo is made with cultured milk, so it's lighter-bodied with a slight tang that plays beautifully against sweet toppings. At Sweet Escape it's self-serve: you pull the handle, build your own topping combination, and pay by weight. It's the most customizable choice — and usually the lighter one.

So which should you get?

  • Craving rich and nostalgic: hand-dipped ice cream.
  • Craving intense flavor in a small package: gelato.
  • Craving something lighter you control completely: frozen yogurt.
  • Avoiding dairy: look for sorbetto in the gelato case.
  • Can't decide: that's the beauty of pay-by-weight — put more than one in the same cup. Nobody here will stop you.

Whatever you pick, the live in-stock menu shows exactly what's in the case right now at our Memphis shop, minutes from Bartlett.

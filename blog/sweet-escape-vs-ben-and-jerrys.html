Dessert shop comparison

Sweet Escape vs. Ben & Jerry’s Scoop Shops

Published September 24, 2026 · Sweet Escape, Memphis

Sweet Escape and Ben & Jerry’s Scoop Shops can both answer an ice cream craving, but they give visitors different ways to make a decision. Ben & Jerry’s Scoop Shops revolve around one famous flavor collection, especially combinations filled with chunks and swirls. Sweet Escape’s Memphis menu stretches across hand-dipped ice cream, self-serve frozen yogurt, gelato, sorbetto, milkshakes, and specialty desserts. The useful question is not which brand wins—it is which menu structure fits what you want today.

Ben & Jerry’s grew from a scoop-shop beginning

Ben & Jerry’s official company history says Ben Cohen and Jerry Greenfield opened their first scoop shop in a renovated gas station in Burlington, Vermont, in 1978. The first franchised shop followed in 1981.

The official Scoop Shops page emphasizes familiar Ben & Jerry’s flavors with chunks and swirls, served in local shops. The page tells visitors to use the shop finder to locate a store rather than promising that every shop carries an identical lineup. Its catering section identifies cups, cones, and sundaes as serving formats, but catering availability is a Ben & Jerry’s service and should not be assumed for Sweet Escape.

One example of that chunk-and-swirl focus has a shop connection: the official history says the first batch of Chocolate Chip Cookie Dough was created in the Burlington scoop shop in 1984 after a customer suggestion, then released in pints in 1991.

Sweet Escape starts with the dessert format

At Sweet Escape, the first decision can be the kind of frozen dessert. The hand-dipped scoop catalog covers ice cream and sherbet. The frozen yogurt catalog documents the self-serve lineup, and the gelato catalog includes gelato and sorbetto. Milkshakes and cookie-based loaded treats appear on the specialties page.

Catalogs are reference tools, not daily inventory guarantees. Check the live in-stock menu close to your trip for the latest published scoop, yogurt, gelato, and sorbetto availability. The complete item directory is the fastest way to browse every documented item and open its detail page. When ingredients, nutrition, or allergens matter, read the published product cautions and ask the shop to verify the current container label; a flavor name cannot establish dietary safety.

Which experience fits your dessert plan?

A Ben & Jerry’s Scoop Shop may fit someone already looking for a particular Ben & Jerry’s flavor or a cup, cone, or sundae built around that brand’s chunk-and-swirl combinations. Sweet Escape may fit a group comparing several dessert formats in one Memphis shop—hand-dipped ice cream for one person, self-serve froyo for another, and gelato or sorbetto for someone else. That is a distinction in selection and planning, not a quality ranking.

Sweet Escape is at 1674 Whitten Rd, Suite 106, Memphis, TN 38134, near Bartlett. Posted hours are Monday–Thursday noon–9 PM and Friday–Sunday noon–10 PM.

Frequently asked questions

What is the main menu difference between Sweet Escape and Ben & Jerry’s Scoop Shops?

Ben & Jerry’s Scoop Shops center the brand’s ice cream flavors, known for chunks and swirls, in scoop-shop servings. Sweet Escape organizes its Memphis menu across hand-dipped ice cream, self-serve frozen yogurt, gelato, sorbetto, milkshakes, and specialty desserts.

Did Ben & Jerry’s begin as a scoop shop?

Yes. Ben & Jerry’s official history says the first shop opened in a renovated gas station in Burlington, Vermont, in 1978.

How can I check Sweet Escape’s current flavors?

Use Sweet Escape’s live in-stock menu for the latest published availability. Its catalogs and item directory document the broader selection.

Is Sweet Escape affiliated with Ben & Jerry’s?

No. Sweet Escape is not affiliated with or endorsed by Ben & Jerry’s.

20 Hand-Dipped Flavors on Sweet Escape's Live Memphis Menu

Published September 9, 2026 · Sweet Escape, Memphis

Wondering which ice cream flavors are available at Sweet Escape in Memphis? The live menu listed 20 hand-dipped choices when this guide was published on September 9, 2026. Because the case can change, think of this article as a dated snapshot and use the current in-stock menu for the latest answer before you visit.

The 20 flavors in the current snapshot

The live scoop list included:

  • Anniversary Cake, Bear Claw, Blue Moon, Blueberry Burst, and Chocolate
  • Cool Mint Cookie, Cotton Candy Twist, Cow Tippin', Michigan Pot Hole, and Mint Chip
  • Pistachio Almond, Puppy Chow, Raspberry Chip Cheesecake, Rush Hour, and Rainbow Sherbet
  • Signature Vanilla, Vanilla Bean, Oregon Strawberry, Peaches & Cream, and Udderly Chocolate

Nineteen of those entries appear in the site's Ice Cream category, while Rainbow Sherbet is labeled Sherbet. That category label is useful for browsing, but it is not a dietary or allergen statement.

Use the names to build a short list

A long menu is easier to browse in small groups. Visitors interested in familiar names might begin with Chocolate, Signature Vanilla, Vanilla Bean, Mint Chip, or Oregon Strawberry. Names such as Anniversary Cake, Blue Moon, Cow Tippin', Michigan Pot Hole, Puppy Chow, and Rush Hour offer a different starting point. If fruit is part of your search, the list also includes Blueberry Burst, Raspberry Chip Cheesecake, Rainbow Sherbet, Oregon Strawberry, and Peaches & Cream.

These groupings are only navigation aids based on the published names. Open the matching cards in the full scoop flavor catalog to read the information actually provided for each product.

Check allergen details one flavor at a time

Similar-sounding flavors do not necessarily carry the same published allergen information. For example, the catalog lists Milk for Blue Moon, Milk and Soy for Mint Chip, and Milk and Almonds for Pistachio Almond. The cards also contain equipment or cross-contact notes where those details were provided. Those examples should not be applied to any other flavor.

Use the online information as a starting point, not a safety guarantee. Ingredients, labels, and handling can change, and some entries do not have complete online details. Read the nutrition and allergen guide, then ask the shop to verify the current container label before ordering when an allergy or dietary need matters.

Why the live menu is the final check

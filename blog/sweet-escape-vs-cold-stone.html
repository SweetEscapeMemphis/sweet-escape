Sweet Escape vs. Cold Stone: Comparing Dessert Formats

Published September 23, 2026 · Sweet Escape, Memphis

Cold Stone Creamery and Sweet Escape can both appear when you search for ice cream around Memphis, yet they organize dessert decisions in different ways. Cold Stone centers a customized ice cream Creation with mix-ins. Sweet Escape gives you several paths: hand-dipped ice cream, self-serve frozen yogurt, gelato, sorbetto, milkshakes, and specialty desserts. Here is a factual look at those formats so you can decide which kind of stop matches what you want.

Cold Stone centers customized ice cream Creations

Cold Stone’s official About Us page describes its signature process as preparing a custom ice cream creation on a frozen granite stone. Its Create Your Own page explains the basic decision: choose an ice cream flavor, then add candy, fruit, nuts, or other mix-ins. That format puts customization inside one ice cream order.

The brand also offers Signature Creations, which its official ice cream menu defines as established combinations of ice cream and mix-ins. Cold Stone’s site lists cakes, smoothies, and shakes as well. Its Create Your Own page distinguishes always-available flavors from seasonal and local-favorite flavors, and notes that some selections vary by store. That means a national menu is useful for understanding the format, but it is not a promise about a particular location.

Sweet Escape starts with a choice of dessert type

Sweet Escape’s menu begins one level earlier: which dessert format sounds right? The scoop catalog covers hand-dipped ice cream and sherbet. The frozen yogurt catalog documents the self-serve side of the shop. A separate gelato and sorbetto catalog makes those choices easy to browse, while the specialties page covers milkshakes and cookie-based loaded desserts.

That broader catalog is not the same as today’s inventory. Before heading to Whitten Road, open the live in-stock menu for the latest published availability. If you want to explore beyond what is currently listed, the complete item directory links the documented scoop, yogurt, gelato, sorbetto, and specialty entries. Flavor names and category labels are not dietary or allergen guarantees, so review the published product details and ask the shop to verify the current container label when that information matters.

Which dessert experience fits your visit?

Cold Stone may fit a craving centered on choosing ice cream and folding in candies, fruit, nuts, or other mix-ins on a frozen stone. Its Signature Creations can also help when you would rather start from a documented combination. Sweet Escape may fit a group that wants to compare different frozen-dessert formats—from a hand-dipped scoop to self-serve froyo, gelato, sorbetto, a milkshake, or a specialty dessert—before everyone makes a choice.

Neither structure is automatically better; they solve different dessert decisions. Sweet Escape is at 1674 Whitten Rd, Suite 106, Memphis, TN 38134, near Bartlett. Posted hours are Monday–Thursday noon–9 PM and Friday–Sunday noon–10 PM.

Frequently asked questions

What is the main difference between Sweet Escape and Cold Stone Creamery?

Cold Stone Creamery emphasizes ice cream customized with mix-ins on a frozen granite stone. Sweet Escape organizes its Memphis menu across hand-dipped ice cream, self-serve frozen yogurt, gelato, sorbetto, milkshakes, and specialty desserts.

Can I check Sweet Escape’s current flavors online?

Yes. Sweet Escape’s live in-stock menu shows the latest published availability, while its catalog and item directory document the broader selection.

Does every Cold Stone store carry the same flavors?

No. Cold Stone’s official Create Your Own page says seasonal and local-favorite selections may vary by store, so visitors should check their chosen location.

Is Sweet Escape affiliated with Cold Stone Creamery?

No. Sweet Escape is not affiliated with or endorsed by Cold Stone Creamery.

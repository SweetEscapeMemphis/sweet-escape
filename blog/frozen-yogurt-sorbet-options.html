Two Sorbet-Labeled Choices in the Frozen Yogurt Lineup

Published September 17, 2026 · Updated September 19, 2026 · Sweet Escape, Memphis

Visitors searching for sorbet choices in Memphis often want two answers: what does Sweet Escape publish about the choice, and how can they check whether the relevant flavors are currently listed? This guide uses the shop’s own menu information and keeps product descriptions separate from changing availability.

What the published menu says

The verified menu describes Pink Lemonade Sorbet and Watermelon Sorbet in the September frozen-yogurt stock list. That wording is the useful starting point because it identifies what the website actually documents without adding assumptions about price, portion options, ingredients, or availability.

Open the specialties menu when the subject is a composed dessert, or the scoop catalog when you need a flavor card. Those pages serve different purposes: one explains menu formats, while the other provides published details for individual scoop flavors.

Check the dated stock snapshot carefully

The website’s stock data was updated September 8, 2026. When this article names a flavor from that list, it is describing that dated snapshot—not promising that the item is available now. Visit the live in-stock menu close to your trip and check the update time displayed there.

The full catalogs are broader than the live list. A catalog entry can remain useful for descriptions, nutrition fields, ingredients, and allergen notes even when the product is not marked in stock. Conversely, current availability should come from the live page rather than from an older article.

Read each product card on its own

Flavor names are navigation clues, not ingredient or dietary guarantees. Two products with similar words can have different published allergen fields, ingredient statements, or cautions. Do not apply one card’s details to another product. The nutrition and allergen guide explains how to read declared allergens and equipment or cross-contact notes.

Online information is only a starting point when an allergy or dietary need matters. Formulas, labels, and handling can change, and some entries do not include complete online data. Ask the shop to verify the current container label before ordering rather than treating a category, flavor name, or blank field as a safety certification.

A simple way to compare your choices

  1. Start with the relevant catalog or specialties page.
  2. Open the live menu and look for the matching item or flavor.
  3. Read the individual product card and all published cautions.
  4. Keep another choice in mind because stock can change.

This sequence makes the website easier to use and avoids mixing permanent catalog information with a rotating menu. For a fuller walkthrough, see the guide to checking the live menu.

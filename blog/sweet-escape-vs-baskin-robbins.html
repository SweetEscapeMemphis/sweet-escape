Sweet Escape vs. Baskin-Robbins: A Memphis Dessert Guide

Published September 22, 2026 · Sweet Escape, Memphis

Choosing a dessert stop is easier when you compare the choices you can actually browse, rather than a brand slogan or a lifetime flavor count. Baskin-Robbins is known for its ice cream flavor history and offers sundaes and ice cream cakes. Sweet Escape’s Memphis website organizes hand-dipped ice cream alongside self-serve frozen yogurt, gelato, sorbetto, milkshakes, and specialty desserts. Here is a fair look at what each brand documents and what to check before you go.

The “31 flavors” idea is history, not a daily inventory

According to Baskin-Robbins’ official fact sheet, the brand began in 1945. Its “31” stood for a different ice cream flavor for every day of the month. Its broader flavor library has since grown far beyond 31. That library is a record of flavors developed over time, not a claim that every flavor will be in every case on a particular day.

Its official online menu includes sundaes, while the brand also documents ice cream cakes. Selection can differ by shop, so a visitor seeking a specific flavor or item should check the relevant store’s current ordering information. We are not making a price, quality, or local inventory comparison here.

Sweet Escape starts with dessert format

At Sweet Escape, you can start with the kind of dessert you want and then browse the documented flavors. The scoop catalog covers hand-dipped ice cream. The frozen yogurt catalog covers the self-serve lineup, and the gelato catalog includes gelato and sorbetto entries. The specialties page documents milkshakes and cookie-based loaded treats. Those are categories on the site, not a promise that every flavor is present on every visit.

For an actual Memphis trip, open the live in-stock menu after browsing the catalogs. It separates the latest published scoop, yogurt, and gelato listings from the larger product catalogs. This matters if one flavor or dietary label is driving your trip. A product name, category filter, or blank allergen field is not a safety guarantee; read the published product cautions and ask the shop to verify the current container label when dietary details matter.

How to choose without a false scorecard

If your search centers on Baskin-Robbins’ flavor program, sundaes, or ice cream cakes, its official menu is the right place to investigate a specific store’s choices. If you want to compare several frozen-dessert formats at one Memphis address, Sweet Escape’s menu pages give you a route through scoops, self-serve yogurt, gelato, sorbetto, and specialties. Both menus deserve to be judged by what is actually offered at the location and time you visit, not by an assumed universal lineup.

Sweet Escape is at 1674 Whitten Rd, Suite 106, Memphis, TN 38134, near Bartlett. Posted hours are Monday–Thursday noon–9 PM and Friday–Sunday noon–10 PM. The Visit page has the phone number and directions, while the live menu is the better starting point for current flavor availability.

Frequently asked questions

Does Baskin-Robbins have only 31 flavors?

No. Its 31 concept originally represented a different flavor for each day of the month. Baskin-Robbins says its flavor library has grown well beyond those original 31; that does not mean every flavor is available at every shop.

What dessert types does Sweet Escape list?

Sweet Escape documents hand-dipped ice cream, self-serve frozen yogurt, gelato, sorbetto, milkshakes, and specialty desserts on its website.

How do I check what Sweet Escape has today?

Check Sweet Escape’s live in-stock menu close to your visit. The flavor catalogs describe products but are not a guarantee of current availability.

Is Sweet Escape affiliated with Baskin-Robbins?

No. Sweet Escape is not affiliated with or endorsed by Baskin-Robbins.

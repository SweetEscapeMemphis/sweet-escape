Two seasonal yogurt records, compared

Pumpkin Pie or Spiced Apple Pie? A Memphis Frozen Yogurt Guide

Published October 7, 2026 · Sweet Escape, Memphis

When fall flavors are on your mind, two Sweet Escape frozen yogurt names may catch your eye: Pumpkin Pie and Spiced Apple Pie. Both are listed as seasonal yogurt flavors, and both have a pie-inspired name. Their published descriptions and ingredient lists, however, give you different details to consider. Here is a straightforward way to compare them before choosing a cup.

The quick distinction: Pumpkin Pie is described with real pumpkin puree and nutmeg, while Spiced Apple Pie is described as baked-apple flavor with pie spices. If the exact ingredients, allergens, or current availability matter to your decision, check the individual product pages and the live menu before visiting.

What the product descriptions say

Pumpkin Pie is described as a classic pie flavor made with real pumpkin puree and nutmeg. Its ingredient record names a pumpkin pie base with pumpkin puree, ground cinnamon, and ground nutmeg, among other ingredients.

Spiced Apple Pie is described as baked-apple flavor warmed with pie spices. Its ingredient list names cinnamon, along with natural and artificial flavors, but does not identify apple as a separate ingredient. The menu description and ingredient statement answer different questions, so it is best to keep both in view rather than fill in recipe details that are not listed.

Those published details offer a practical starting point: choose Pumpkin Pie if the product’s documented pumpkin and nutmeg details interest you; choose Spiced Apple Pie if its baked-apple description is closer to what you are looking for. Descriptions help you browse. They do not guarantee that you will experience a flavor in a particular way, and they are not complete substitutes for an ingredient label.

Read the ingredient notes for the exact flavor

The product records share several base ingredients, including milk, liquid sugar, corn syrup, nonfat milk, whey, and cream. Both also list the same five active yogurt cultures: S. thermophilus, L. bulgaricus, L. acidophilus, B. bifidus, and L. casei. The flavor-specific parts differ.

Pumpkin Pie’s ingredient list includes pumpkin puree, sweetened condensed skim milk, cinnamon, nutmeg, annatto extract, caramel color, and Red 40 in its pumpkin pie base. Spiced Apple Pie’s list includes natural and artificial flavors, caramel color, citric acid, xanthan gum, Yellow 6, and cinnamon. This comparison is drawn from the published records; it does not confirm the complete recipe beyond what those records state.

Both product pages declare milk and carry the same facility statement: the products are made in a facility that also processes peanuts, tree nuts, eggs, soy, milk, and wheat products. A declared-allergen table and a facility note are separate pieces of information. If an allergy or dietary need matters, read the current label and ask Sweet Escape to verify it. Do not use a flavor name or an incomplete online detail as a safety guarantee.

Which one should you explore first?

You do not need to decide based on which pie you like more in general. Start with the detail that matters to you today:

  • Want the entry whose description names pumpkin puree? Open Pumpkin Pie and review its published product information.
  • Interested in the baked-apple description? Open Spiced Apple Pie and note that the online ingredient record does not separately name apple.
  • Checking an allergy or ingredient? Compare the current container label for the exact flavor, then ask the shop if anything needs clarification.
  • Planning around what is being served? Check the live in-stock list rather than relying on a catalog page or an older menu snapshot.

Sweet Escape also lists Ooey Gooey Cinnamon Bun as another seasonal yogurt flavor. Its published description highlights cinnamon-bun flavor and sweet cream-cheese icing notes. Its ingredient record includes egg as well as milk, so keep its separate details attached if you compare all three options.

Availability is tied to the dated live list

When checked for this October 7 article, Sweet Escape’s live stock file showed a last-updated time of October 4, 2026, and listed both Pumpkin Pie and Spiced Apple Pie in its yogurt section. That is a dated snapshot, not a promise that either flavor will remain available later. The catalog records describe products; the live menu is the place to check the shop’s latest published lineup.

For a Memphis visit, check the live menu close to your trip, then open the product page for the exact flavor you are considering. If an ingredient answer will determine your choice, contact the shop using the information on the visit page. You can then make your decision from the current details, with both the flavor description and the product-specific cautions in front of you.

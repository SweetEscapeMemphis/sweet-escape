Bringing a Group to Sweet Escape: Birthdays, Teams & Celebrations

Published August 28, 2026 · Sweet Escape, Memphis

Some of our favorite moments at the shop are the loud ones — a birthday crew debating toppings, a youth team celebrating a win, three generations of one family comparing cones. If you're planning a group dessert stop in the Memphis or Bartlett area, here's how to make it smooth.

Why pay-by-weight works for groups

Group orders usually stall at the register while everyone decides on sizes. Our setup skips that: everyone grabs a cup, builds their own treat — scoops, self-serve froyo with toppings, gelato — and each cup is weighed. Kids love the independence; whoever's paying loves that a light cup costs a light price.

Tips for birthday and team visits

  • Call ahead for bigger groups. A quick call to (901) 718-5812 lets us know you're coming and helps us point you to the best time.
  • Weeknights are calmer. Monday–Thursday we're open 12–9 PM, and evenings midweek are usually easier for a crowd than a Saturday night rush.
  • Check the case before you promise a flavor. The lineup rotates — the live menu shows exactly what's in stock today, so there are no birthday-kid disappointments.
  • Allergies in the group? Our nutrition and allergen notes cover every flavor, and the team can help you double-check at the counter.

Easy to reach from Memphis and Bartlett

We're at 1674 Whitten Rd, Suite 106 in the Goodlett Commons shopping center — an easy stop after games, practices, school events, or dinner anywhere in northeast Memphis or Bartlett, with parking right outside the door.

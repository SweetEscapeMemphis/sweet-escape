Planning a Group Dessert Stop at Sweet Escape

Published August 28, 2026 · Updated August 31, 2026 · Sweet Escape, Memphis

A group dessert stop involves more preferences than a solo visit. Sweet Escape lists hand-dipped ice cream, self-serve frozen yogurt with toppings, gelato, sorbetto, milkshakes, and cookie-based loaded treats. Sharing the right pages before the trip lets everyone see the choices without assuming that a particular flavor or service will be available.

Share the current menu first

Begin with the live in-stock menu. It is designed to show the scoop, yogurt, and gelato flavors currently listed by the shop, while the larger catalog pages show the broader range. Because the lineup rotates, check again close to the visit if someone is interested in one specific flavor.

Plan around a rotating lineup

For a group, it helps to share both the live menu and the relevant catalog pages. The live menu answers the availability question; the catalogs give people more room to search descriptions and published product details. Keeping those jobs separate avoids the common mistake of treating every catalog flavor as available on the same day.

Ask anyone with a must-have flavor to choose a backup as well. A backup does not need to be an exact substitute: someone looking at hand-dipped ice cream might also review the gelato list, while someone interested in self-serve yogurt can compare more than one current flavor. This is a planning tip, not a promise about how orders, portions, or pricing work in the shop. Sending the links ahead also gives people time to read the available labels.

Give everyone a useful starting point

  • Hand-dipped choices: use the scoop flavor catalog.
  • Self-serve frozen yogurt: compare flavors and published details on the yogurt page.
  • Gelato and sorbetto: search the separate gelato catalog.
  • Current visit details: use the Visit page for the address, regular hours, phone number, and directions.

Review ingredient and allergen information

The product pages include available nutrition facts, ingredient lists, and declared allergen details. They also flag situations where information is limited or a current container label should be checked. If anyone in the group has a dietary or allergy concern, read those cautions carefully and contact the shop for current information. The website does not guarantee that a product is suitable for an individual need or free from cross-contact.

Confirm logistics that matter to your group

The website does not publish group capacity, reservations, party packages, parking details, or preferred times for larger visits. If any of those details affect your plans, call (901) 718-5812 before leaving rather than relying on an assumption. Regular hours are Monday–Thursday 12–9 PM and Friday–Sunday 12–10 PM.

Find the Memphis shop

Sweet Escape is at 1674 Whitten Rd, Suite 106, Memphis, TN 38134, near Bartlett. The Visit page includes the map and a direct link for turn-by-turn directions.

How it works

Three steps, zero hassle

  1. Join at the register

    On your next visit, tell us your phone number at checkout. That's the whole signup — free, and it takes seconds.

  2. Earn points every purchase

    Check in with your phone number each time you pay and your points add up automatically — scoops, froyo, gelato, shakes, all of it counts.

  3. Redeem for free treats

    When you hit a reward, we'll let you know at the register. Cash in your points whenever you're ready.

Good to know

Rewards questions, answered

How do I join?
Give your phone number at the register on your next visit to 1674 Whitten Rd, Suite 106. No forms, no card to carry.
Does it cost anything?
Nope — membership is completely free.
How do I check my points?
Ask at the register any time, or download the free Clover app for iPhone or Android, search for Sweet Escape, and see your points and available rewards right on your phone.
What if I forget to check in?
Just mention it at the register — your points are tied to your phone number, so checking in on your next purchase keeps them adding up.

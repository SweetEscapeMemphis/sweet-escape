## Sources checked November 27, 2026

- McCollum [US22966A](https://patents.google.com/patent/US22966A/en), 1859: steam escape, dough release plate, flattening and docking in sequence before baking. Extons [US164283A](https://patents.google.com/patent/US164283A/en), 1875: roller points on part of its circumference, forming and docking in the same pass. [US2887964A](https://patents.google.com/patent/US2887964A/en), 1959, figs. 6–7 and 27–33: separate scoring bars and radially oriented docking pins on a rotary cutter. These are examples, not claims of invention priority or industry-wide usage.
- [US6716462B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US6716462B2/en): venting and point bonding proposed; pressed pieces described in the patent’s conditions. [US5747092A](https://patents.google.com/patent/US5747092A/en): examples of intentional pillowing and a flat base with some blisters, not health or taste claims. [WO2008028112A2](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2008028112A2/en), paragraph 2, identifies steam and leavening gas as potential contributors. [WO2014210006A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2014210006A1/en), paragraphs 18–20, describes full and partial punctures and partially reclosing examples. No universal formula, hole depth or count claimed.
- [Baking Industry Research Trust cracker information sheet](https://www.bakeinfo.co.nz/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/BakeInfo_Info_Crackers.pdf), pp. 1–2: 3×3 docking pattern and separate sheet-breaking lines in the soda example; two dots in the round snack example. Used as a contrast, not as a graham cracker standard. Authors’ original recipes [Gale Gand and Julia Moskin](https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/60s-indoor-smores-with-homemade-graham-crackers-recipe-1938974) and [King Arthur whole-wheat grahams](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/100-whole-wheat-graham-crackers-recipe) specify different fork timing. Suggested paired observation is an option where the recipe permits, not a reported experiment.
- [King Arthur Baking pastry prebake](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2026/09/01/prebake-blind-bake-pie-crust): weighted stage and the subsequent docking step for a full prebake. [Rossi Anastopoulo’s easy ice cream pie](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/easy-ice-cream-pie-recipe): baked crackers as starting crumbs; melted butter, pressed crust, and no additional baking. Neither is the shop’s recipe, and no third-party directions reproduced.

## Sources checked October 23

- https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/how-to-interpret-a-false-color-satellite-image/ — NASA explanation of absorbed/reflected wavelengths and chlorophyll-rich vegetation. General optics, not a dessert measurement.

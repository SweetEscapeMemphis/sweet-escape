## Authoritative sources and factual limits

1. GoTürkiye, Kahramanmaraş taste guide: https://goturkiye.com/kahramanmaras/taste — regional home, traditional milk/sahlep/sugar and goat’s milk; heated/stirred/rested/cooled overview. Does not establish one recipe for every Turkish product. No exact cooking directions or traditional milk sourcing claims applied to Sweet Escape.
2. Kew, From pods to puddings, 2022: https://www.kew.org/read-and-watch/vanilla-digitisation — certain orchids’ tuber powder used as thickener in dondurma; separate cured vanilla fruit pods. No flowers-as-ingredient inference, commercial species counts, prices, historical vanillin or popularity claims used.
3. Kurt & Kahyaoglu 2017, Carbohydrate Polymers 168, 138–146, DOI 10.1016/j.carbpol.2017.03.060: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0144861717303168 — publisher indexed introduction identifies linear glucose/mannose chains. Direct publisher access returned 403; no paywall bypass. University-author record checked: https://avesis.yildiz.edu.tr/yayin/5d6dbb91-0b82-4711-b03f-eff9d2801ac1/purification-of-glucomannan-from-salep-part-1-detailed-rheological-characteristics . No purification method, medical use or generic gelation threshold transferred into food advice.
4. NLM MeSH, Viscoelastic Substances: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/mesh/68055682 — viscosity and elasticity, time-dependent response to force. USDA NALT viscosity definition also checked: https://lod.nal.usda.gov/nalt/en/page/57054?anylang=on&clang=en . NIST practice-guide indexed definition corroborates viscous response and elastic recovery: https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=850489 . Only generic definitions, not a medical claim or claim of complete recovery for every ice cream.
5. Arslan et al. 2023, Food Science and Technology 43, DOI 10.1590/fst.103822: https://www.scielo.br/j/cta/a/XSPQDQKR6sv7ddXShhfPrZm/?lang=en — primary full HTML abstract, methods 2.3 and results 3.2 checked. Ice-cream mixtures measured using flow, oscillatory and 3-ITT tests at 25°C; salep samples differ; shear-thinning, solid-like viscoelastic/recoverable behavior limited to study conditions. A rheometer result is not a direct measurement of every finished frozen serving’s stretch.
6. Turkmen et al. 2021, Journal of Food Processing and Preservation, e16063, DOI 10.1111/jfpp.16063: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/jfpp.16063 — primary abstract, ten orchid species, salep composition and salep versus stabilizer-emulsifier formulations, differences in hardness/melting/rheology. No universal equivalence of powders, quantitative sensory ranking or sustainable-sourcing endorsement.
7. Kanca 2023, Journal of Food and Nutrition Research 62, 129–139: https://vup.sk/en/index.php?article=2308&mainID=2&navID=34&version=2&volume=62 — publisher HTML summary: Orchis sancta model mixtures, concentration/heat, water holding and apparent viscosity; more uniform structure from 70–80°C, clusters at 90°C for the specified conditions. Not a cooking recipe or universal optimal temperature.
8. H. Douglas Goff, University of Guelph: https://books.lib.uoguelph.ca/icecreamtechnologyebook/chapter/stabilizers/ — official indexed chapter describes viscosity in mix/unfrozen phase, crystal-growth management and excess viscosity/heavy-chewy limits. https://books.lib.uoguelph.ca/icecreamtechnologyebook/chapter/ice-cream-meltdown/ — official indexed chapter separates ice melting from fat-stabilized foam collapse and notes external heat-transfer conditions. Direct opens 403; no access-control bypass. General principles only, not proof of a particular Maraş or Sweet Escape formula, melting rate or safety.
9. Kew, Orchidelirium, February 19, 2026: https://www.kew.org/read-and-watch/orchidelirium — current institutional conservation discussion identifies food/medicine collection pressures and salep/dondurma. No international-trade/legal guidance, harvest estimates or species-extinction percentages asserted. No harvesting tutorial.

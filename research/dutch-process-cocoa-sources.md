# Dutch-process cocoa color — sources

Checked November 16, 2026. Canonical: https://www.sweetescapememphis.com/blog/why-dutch-process-cocoa-is-darker.html

- [American Society of Baking on cocoa powder](https://asbe.org/article/cocoa-powder/?topic=ingredient) and [Van Houten brand history](https://www.barry-callebaut.com/en-US/vending/van-houten/history): powder/cocoa-butter separation versus treatment with alkaline salts. Family association only; no single inventor/date attributed to both processes.
- [Stanley et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13158974/), Table 3: untreated L 32.9 and pH 5.57; heat-only L 31.3 and pH 5.57; treated L roughly 29 and pH around 7.5–8.0. Lightness and hue channels, controlled comparisons, not a recipe or general quantitative claim.
- [Miller et al. commercial powders](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18710243/): red and brown/black treated groups. [King Arthur’s baking trials](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2020/07/15/dutch-process-vs-natural-cocoa): natural vs Dutch/black/rouge descriptions and the dependence of baking-soda substitution on the other ingredients.
- [Dark brown natural cocoa disclosure, EP3013154B1](https://patents.google.com/patent/EP3013154B1/en), Example 1/Tables 2–3: water-treated test samples pH 5.4–5.7, two lightness results between the document’s example red and dark alkali-treated references. Reported slurry values only; not evidence of widespread usage, product equivalence or shop process.
- [21 CFR 163.112](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-163/subpart-B/section-163.112) and [163.113](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-163/subpart-B/section-163.113): wording about alkali processing on the standardized cocoa package as of check date; not a statement about finished desserts or a regulatory compliance review.
- [PJ Hamel’s Chocolate Decadence Ice Cream](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/chocolate-decadence-ice-cream-recipe): Dutch-process powder and separately melted chopped chocolate both enter the base. Not a recipe used by the shop.
- Shop: Belgian Dark Chocolate gelato page and `data/gelato-flavors.js`; milk and egg, possible wheat, tree nuts, peanuts and soy. Current ingredient/container/nutrition/facility/equipment/handling/cross-contact cautions. Cocoa powder type and process not stated in catalog; no availability or formulation inferred.

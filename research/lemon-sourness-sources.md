## Authoritative sources checked October 24

- https://today.usc.edu/how-sour-tastes-otop1-protein-humans-mice-usc-research/ — USC original research account (2019); citric acid in lemons, other food acids, hydrogen ions and OTOP1. No fruit-color mechanism or universal sensory-intensity claim.
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7299528/ — Teng et al., Cellular and neural responses to sour stimuli require the proton channel Otop1, Current Biology 2019, DOI 10.1016/j.cub.2019.08.077. Primary cell/nerve measurements in mice; loss of proton currents and attenuated acid responses. Explicitly not a human dessert preference trial.
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2637791/ — Penniston et al. 2008, DOI 10.1089/end.2007.0304. Ion-chromatography analysis of specific fresh juices and commercial products; samples and variation distinguished from all current foods. Only analytical chemistry discussed; no medical or health claims.
- https://www.epa.gov/caddis/ph — EPA definition of logarithmic pH, hydrogen-ion activity versus concentration. A one-unit chemical difference is not asserted to be tenfold perceived sourness. No shop pH invented.
- https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/BF03194908 — Savant and McDaniel 2004 primary publisher abstract; trained panelists, acids/sugars, sweetness matching versus equal mass/molar quantities. Perceptual suppression distinguished from chemical removal of acid. No paywall bypass or full text claim.
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7600934/ — Junge et al. 2020, DOI 10.3390/foods9101425; primary indexed study text and Aarhus author-institution record reviewed. Direct PMC extraction encountered browser check; no challenge bypass. Sucrose/acid aqueous mixtures, overall mutual suppression and individual perceptual clusters. No national preference stereotype or universal recipe claim.
- https://www.nidcd.nih.gov/health/taste-disorders — NIDCD taste/flavor explanation: at least five basic tastes, aroma/temperature/texture and dispersed taste-cell types. Only general sensory biology used, no medical advice.
- Original prose, brief source-specific explanations, no copied third-party article paragraphs or invented quotes/experiences.

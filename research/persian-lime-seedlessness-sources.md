## Sources checked November 25, 2026

- UC Riverside Citrus Variety Collection [Mexican lime accession](https://citrusvariety.ucr.edu/crc1710) and [Bearss lime accession](https://citrusvariety.ucr.edu/crc3772): fruit appearance, ripening, name conventions, 1987 accession comparison, triploidy and rare seeds. These collection observations are not guarantees about individual store fruit or size. [UC Cooperative Extension, Kallsen](https://ucanr.edu/sites/default/files/2014-01/179473.pdf), page 4: Tahiti limes can set fruit without pollination. [UC Riverside budwood guidance](https://citrusvariety.ucr.edu/purchase-budwood-rootstocks-seedlings): true-to-type citrus budwood and rootstock; no home grafting instructions.
- Curk et al., [Phylogenetic origin of limes and lemons](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4817432/) (2016): leaf samples from 133 citrus accessions; nuclear and cytoplasmic markers; proposed lemon ovule and Mexican-like contribution to triploid Tahiti/Persian types. Ahmed et al., [Origin of triploid limes](https://academic.oup.com/aob/article/121/3/571/4775246) (2018): test of contribution and segregation through modern experimental crosses, consistent with the model but not a record of the historical cross. [NC State Mountain Horticultural Crops Research and Extension Center](https://mountainhort.ces.ncsu.edu/developing-non-invasive-nursery-crops/): mechanism for low fertility and limits of the odd-sets analogy. Fruit size and seedlessness should not be treated as interchangeable consequences of ploidy.
- [Lime essential-oil GC/MS study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6770194/) (2019): dozens of measured volatile compounds, including limonene, gamma-terpinene and beta-pinene. The comparison is limited to the analyzed oil; no health, batch, ranking, or intensity claims. [FoodSafety.gov](https://www.foodsafety.gov/blog/safe-ways-handle-and-clean-produce): rinse fresh produce before cutting.
- Original recipes: [Fany Gerson’s Oaxacan lime sorbet](https://www.penguinrandomhouseretail.com/book/?isbn=9781607747772) (publisher excerpt) contains zest, no lime juice; [Alton Brown’s Key Lime Sorbet](https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/alton-brown/key-lime-sorbet-recipe-1938398) specifies juice plus zest; [Jennifer Segal’s no-churn Key Lime Pie ice cream](https://www.onceuponachef.com/recipes/no-churn-key-lime-pie-ice-cream.html) uses regular limes by choice, both juice and zest, and separate baked graham crust pieces. Method contrasts only; no recipe reproduction, claim about origins or ranking, preparation or endorsement for the local scoop.

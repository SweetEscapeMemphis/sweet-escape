# Coconut eyes and germination — sources

Checked November 17, 2026. Canonical: https://www.sweetescapememphis.com/blog/why-coconuts-have-three-eyes.html

- [Plant Resources of South-East Asia](https://prosea.prota4u.org/view.aspx?id=829), [Beveridge et al. 2022, Fruit Biology of Coconut](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9738799/), including [figures](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36501334/): fruit layers, drupe, single seed, carpel fusion, softer eye, embryo and sequence of outward tissue, root and shoot; inward haustorium. No guarantee about the viability of a purchased fruit.
- [Li et al., Agronomy Journal](https://acsess.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2134/agronj2019.02.0137): measurements in Hainan Tall and Red Dwarf during germination and interpretation of liquid stores early and solid reserves later. [López-Villalobos et al. 2001](https://academic.oup.com/jxb/article/52/358/933/597150): intact vs isolated embryos, respective growth patterns and differing fatty-acid accumulation. Intact haustoria reached 45 g per nut over the 120-day experiment in that study; not a universal size or timetable.
- [Thilakarathne et al. 2025](https://ir.lib.pdn.ac.lk/items/cc5cf5db-b9f7-4564-b928-903c609e31e2): seven king coconut trees, size and endosperm comparisons; roughly seven months to full external size and solid lining still developing up to eight months in their sample. No inference about other varieties or the named scoop.
- [Yong et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6255029/): coconut water is liquid endosperm, coconut milk is made from grated solid endosperm. Used for terminology only; no dietary, nutritional or health-benefit inference.
- Existing `scoops/pina-coolada/index.html`, `data/flavors.js`, live stock data and directory reviewed. Exact catalog description is coconut ice cream with pineapple chunks; no statement of coconut form or sprouted coconut. Lists milk and coconut, with shared-equipment note for peanuts, other tree nuts, wheat, soy and eggs. No inventory claim.

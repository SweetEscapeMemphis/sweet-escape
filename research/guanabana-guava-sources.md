## Fruit identification sources

- UF/IFAS Gardening Solutions, [Atemoya — Sweetsop, Soursop and Atemoya](https://gardeningsolutions.ifas.ufl.edu/plants/edibles/fruits/atemoya/). Identifies soursop, graviola, and guanabana as common names for *Annona muricata*; distinguishes sugar apple/sweetsop (*Annona squamosa*) and cherimoya (*Annona cherimola*); gives general soursop context. Used for botanical background only, not to describe Sweet Escape's recipe or flavor experience.
- UF/IFAS Gardening Solutions, [Guava](https://gardeningsolutions.ifas.ufl.edu/plants/edibles/fruits/guava/). Identifies guava as *Psidium guajava* and notes that fruit characteristics vary among cultivars. Used only for general fruit identification, not for inferring Sweet Escape's product formulation or sensory qualities.
- Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations, [Principles and Practices of Small- and Medium-Scale Fruit Juice Processing, section 15.6: Guanabana (Soursop)](https://www.fao.org/4/y2515e/y2515e17.htm). Gives *Annona muricata* as the botanical name and discusses guanabana puree and frozen-dessert contexts. Used as general food-use context, not as a manufacturing claim about the Sweet Escape item.

# Sculpted ice cream — source and publication notes

Checked November 15, 2026. Canonical: https://www.sweetescapememphis.com/blog/george-washington-ice-cream-mold.html

- [Dixon Gallery and Gardens, George Washington Ice Cream Mold](https://www.dixon.org/george-washington-ice-cream-mold): New York maker Eppelsheimer & Co., early twentieth-century pewter, object 1991.DA.10.29.3, stamp 1084, photographs of both halves, Washington’s stated yield, two separately molded Liberty pieces, plaster Cupid figure (qualified attribution), display models and lead warning. The object is in the collection; current exhibit status is not claimed. Four fluid ounces is arithmetic from stated catalog yield, not a measurement.
- [Smithsonian, brick mold](https://americanhistory.si.edu/it/collections/object/nmah_870279), [asparagus mold](https://americanhistory.si.edu/collections/object/nmah_676546), [dolphin mold](https://www.si.edu/object/dolphin-shaped-ice-cream-mold%3Anmah_1297170): object records for the Hendler collection’s one-quart block, asparagus silhouette and an Eppelsheimer dolphin, with the dolphin record discussing coloring details and lead-containing pewter. No flavor inferred from a shape.
- [London Museum, Agnes Marshall](https://www.londonmuseum.org.uk/collections/london-stories/agnes-marshall-victorian-queen-of-ice-cream/) and [Wellcome Collection, 1885 Book of Ices](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/kz7nkrvw): pictured shapes and the book’s broad range of frozen dishes, not all dairy ice cream.
- [Cassell US1560205A](https://patents.google.com/patent/US1560205A/en): November 1925, description and drawings for slipper and heart inserts inside a larger ice-cream block. A disclosed design, not evidence of widespread use. The article explains negative/positive relief and slicing geometry with original examples.
- [FDA, Lead in Food and Foodwares](https://www.fda.gov/food/environmental-contaminants-food/lead-food-and-foodwares): risk context for leaving antique pewter out of food preparation.
- Shop connection: `specialties/brownie-base/index.html` confirms warm brownie with choice of one scoop, as a separate, present-day arrangement. Existing social preview shows that sundae, not a museum object. No museum affiliation, shape availability, stock, recipe inference, or safety assurance.

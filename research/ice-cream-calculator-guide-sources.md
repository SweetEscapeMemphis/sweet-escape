## Verified calculator details

- Container estimator: editable light (1/3 cup), standard (1/2 cup), and generous (2/3 cup) serving assumptions; 0–30% extra; pint (2 cups), quart (4), half gallon (8), gallon (16); container count rounds up to whole units.
- Default container example: 20 guests × 1/2 cup × 1.1 = 11 cups required; 3 quarts = 12 cups purchased. This is a calculation example, not a verified in-store portion.
- Optional container costs use user-entered price and supplies amounts. Blank cost entries are excluded from the total.
- Optional dry-topping estimate defaults to 15 g per guest, is editable from 0–100 g per guest, and applies the extra allowance. Sauces are not quantified.
- Sweet Escape by-weight estimator: 5 oz per scoop, optional 4 oz Cookie Dough Base or Brownie Base per guest independent of scoop count, 0–30% extra, and the current calculator-displayed rate of $0.80 per weight ounce.
- Default by-weight example: 20 guests × 1 scoop × 5 oz × 1.1 = 110 weight oz; at the displayed $0.80/oz rate, the calculation is $88 before tax and other expenses. Actual serving weights may vary.
- The page explicitly distinguishes weight ounces from fluid ounces and does not convert container volume to weight. Sharing saves the inputs, not checklist checkmarks.

## Scope and cautions

The article explains two different planning tools and their displayed assumptions. It does not turn the general container estimate into Sweet Escape pricing, represent the weight estimate as a guaranteed order quote, imply that Sweet Escape provides event, catering, delivery, reservation, or bulk services, or claim that the tools confirm stock. It makes no product availability, nutrition, ingredient, or dietary-suitability claims. No third-party facts were used.

Lights/candles, 124 waiters, illuminated sculptured ice cream, clear-ice City Hall models/letters and internally lighted eagle. Colored lights and small batteries belong to a historical display, not a DIY food suggestion. Browser-visible text read; web text fetch alone returned 403. No challenge/security bypass or invented direct participation. No medical research or treatment claims adopted.

City of Stockholm, Architecture: https://stadshuset.stockholm/en/explore-the-city-hall/architecture/ — Blue Hall as covered inner courtyard, Venetian influence, large marble staircase and comparison to Scala dei Giganti. Used as setting/context, not evidence of a dessert recipe or a claim that the hall is painted blue.

Ulrica Söderlind, The Nobel Banquets — a century of culinary history: https://www.nobelprize.org/ceremonies/nobel-banquets-a-century-of-culinary-history/ — researcher/author's account identifies the named Nobel parfait's first serving in 1976, inner and outer flavors, later theatrical stairway presentation. Browser-visible text read after web fetch 403. The 1950 display is explicitly not equated with this 1976 named parfait. No historical staff counts carried forward as current numbers. 4. Nobel's official 2000 video summary: https://www.nobelprize.org/ceremonies/the-nobel-banquet-2000-2000/ — describes ice-cream parade in a candlelit hall, Blue Hall dinner and Golden Hall dancing. Dated event record, not future menu assurance.
5. Nobel's official 2001 video summary: https://www.nobelprize.org/ceremonies/the-nobel-banquet-2001-2001/ — visible browser text independently read, description identifies parade at 8:46 and later dancing. Timestamp belongs to this archived summary, not another video or a claim about all banquets. Video description, not invented personal attendance or claim of viewing unobserved frames.

7. Official 2001 menu: https://www.nobelprize.org/ceremonies/nobel-banquet-menu-2001/ — vanilla ice cream/blackcurrant parfait/meringue duo. Primary full menu search excerpt reviewed. Not treated as complete ingredients, process, weights or nutrition.

Nobel Prize Museum's own Bistro Nobel page: https://www.nobelprizemuseum.se/en/plan-your-visit/bistro-nobel/ — vanilla ice cream, blackcurrant sorbet, raspberry sauce, chocolate medal and spun sugar; chairs signed by laureates as a guestbook. Selected components: Swedish blackthorn-berry sorbet with orange/allspice/clove, baked fresh-cheese cream/vanilla, browned-butter sponge, buttermilk caramel, buckwheat/oat crunch. No general claim about all sorbet ingredients or dairy-free safety. Historical 2025 menu, no unheld 2026 banquet prediction. No recipes or quantities reproduced.

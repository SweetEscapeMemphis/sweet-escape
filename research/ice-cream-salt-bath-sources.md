# Why salt helps make ice cream: source notes

Research checked October 17, 2026, for `/blog/why-salt-helps-make-ice-cream.html`.

## Claim scope

- The article explains the general ice-and-salt bath used in educational and traditional home ice-cream methods. It does not claim Sweet Escape uses that method.
- Salt dissolved in water lowers the solution's freezing point. Melting ice in the bath absorbs energy from nearby surroundings; the colder bath can then draw heat through a sealed inner container.
- The exact bath temperature depends on the mixture and conditions. The article gives no invented recipe quantities, temperature guarantee, or food-safety promise.
- The salt belongs to the outside bath in the described setup, not to the ice-cream mixture. The article recommends following a reputable recipe and using food-grade materials.

Its facilitator notes distinguish pure-water equilibrium from a salty brine bath.

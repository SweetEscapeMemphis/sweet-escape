# Cactus pear fruit — sources and publication record

Checked November 18, 2026. Canonical: https://www.sweetescapememphis.com/blog/what-is-cactus-pear-ice-cream.html

- [Missouri Department of Conservation](https://mdc.mo.gov/discover-nature/field-guide/eastern-prickly-pear): distinct eastern species, pear-shaped fruit, transient true leaves, flattened stems and areoles. Not evidence of the shop's fruit source.
- [Kew: common pear](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/30065762-2) and [Kew: cultivated prickly pear](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/1151735-2): Pyrus communis in Rosaceae versus Opuntia ficus-indica in Cactaceae. [FAO crop profile](https://ecocrop.apps.fao.org/ecocrop/srv/en/cropView?id=8094): alternate names and young stem use. [NPS](https://home.nps.gov/colm/learn/nature/prickly-pear.htm): photos of pad, flower and fruit and botanical context.
- [UC Davis on nopalitos](https://postharvest.ucdavis.edu/produce-facts-sheets/nopalitos): vegetable is the young stem. [Nevada Extension](https://extension.unr.edu/publication.aspx?PubID=2157): tuna versus nopales, easily detached barbed glochids and their presence without large spines. No wild-foraging or spine-removal instructions.
- [UC Davis cactus pear](https://postharvest.ucdavis.edu/ar/produce-facts-sheets/cactus-prickly-pear) and [orchard pear](https://fruitsandnuts.ucdavis.edu/pear-harvest-postharvest): cultivar colors, maturity cues and contrasting harvest conventions. No storage-life or safety promise.
- [Castellanos-Santiago and Yahia, 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18578538/): ten Mexican cultivars/lines; pigment amounts and ratios differed and Camuesa had the highest total betalains in this set. No health or finished-product color inference.
- [Morphological diversity of Mexican xoconostles, original study](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/7FA69E5279C6331EAD153988A4C1F69B/S0248129412000011a.pdf/morphological_diversity_of_xoconostles_opuntia_spp_or_acidic_cactus_pears_a_mexican_contribution_to_functional_foods.pdf): Fig. 1 contrasts distributed seeds in sweet-fruit pulp with xoconostle's thick edible mesocarp and distinct seed core. No nutritional or health claims.
- [Arizona Extension](https://extension.arizona.edu/sites/extension.arizona.edu/files/pubs/az1800-2019.pdf): prepared fruit as topping, juice/syrup distinction and seeded pulp. [Mexican agriculture ministry, Hidalgo](https://www.gob.mx/agricultura/hidalgo/articulos/la-tuna-una-fruta-excelente-y-de-exquisito-sabor?idiom=es): ice cream among established culinary uses (indexed official excerpt; browser fetch denied).

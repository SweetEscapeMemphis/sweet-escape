## Sources checked November 25, 2026

- [Taylor 8756 documentation](https://www.taylor-company.com/equipment/equipment-detail/model-8756/) gives two freezing cylinders and either flavor or their twin twist. H.C. Duke's [Electro Freeze operator's manual](https://www.partstown.com/modelManual/EF-SLX400C_spm.pdf), printed pp. 10–11 / figures 5-1 and 5-2, describes the side and central plungers, two cylinders, serrated nozzle and the separate rotating beater. [Manufacturer's current product page](https://electrofreeze.com/equipments/slx400c/) confirms the model. These are examples only, not assertions about shop equipment or all machines.
- [1954 US2673675A](https://patents.google.com/patent/US2673675A/en), claim and drawings: different feeds into nested inner and outer tubes for filling receptacles, contrasted with visible side-by-side stripes. [US20150296832A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20150296832A1/en), especially paragraphs 48–50: round fill versus fluted top, an eight-point outlet and eight corresponding ridges in the example. These reports are design evidence, not proof of origin or general practice.
- [Taylor UK illustrated serving guide](https://www.taylor-company.co.uk/expert-advice-get-that-perfect-peak/) explains cone lowering and the slight lift and pullaway behind a peak. The separate fixed-outlet/orbit example is geometric reasoning rather than a universal manual or claim about machinery; [King Arthur Baking's multiple-color bag example](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2021/04/08/how-to-use-a-pastry-bag) grounds the two-stream analogy and changeable tips. The experimental straight/coil and round/star comparisons are illustrative, not reported trial results.
- [H. Douglas Goff, Freezing/Whipping of Ice Cream](https://books.lib.uoguelph.ca/icecreamtechnologyebook/chapter/freezing-whipping-of-ice-cream/): partially frozen draw, air incorporation and hardening distinction. [2024 Food Hydrocolloids paper, DOI 10.1016/j.foodhyd.2024.110038](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0268005X24003126): experimental modified starch formulations, extrusion and self-support, 0.95 mm line for one formulation. No extrapolation of laboratory dimensions, formula or holding behavior to a shop. The ridge-rounded-before-dripping possibility is consistent with [experimental imaging of ice cream meltdown](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11189849/); five minutes in the article is illustrative, not a measured guarantee.

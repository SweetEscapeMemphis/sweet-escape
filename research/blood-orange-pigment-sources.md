- UC Riverside Citrus Variety Collection: [Moro](https://citrusvariety.ucr.edu/crc3811) distinguishes rind blush and flesh; [Thermal Tarocco](https://citrusvariety.ucr.edu/crc4018) records pigmentation differences among three selections at Riverside; [Cara Cara](https://citrusvariety.ucr.edu/crc3994) identifies lycopene as a carotenoid behind deep pink flesh.
- [Butelli et al. 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22427337/), original abstract and indexed publisher text: naturally arising mobile DNA near Ruby, cold-dependent pigment regulation, independent Chinese-origin example. No protocols or universal mechanism claims.
- [Carmona et al. 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28764055/): original abstract, harvested Moro versus Pera, two cool storage temperatures, darker Moro flesh and greater anthocyanins at the warmer of the treatments. [Habibi et al. 2020](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3921/9/10/1016): four cultivars, two temperatures, individual and total anthocyanins. No instructions for storage, freezing or color manipulation; no health claims.

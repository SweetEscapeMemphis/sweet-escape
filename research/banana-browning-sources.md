## Verified sources (checked October 28)

- IFST, [Fruit and vegetables: enzymic browning](https://www.ifst.org/lovefoodlovescience/resources/fruit-and-vegetables-enzymic-browning). Tissue disruption, oxygen contact, enzyme catalysts and the apple example. Scope is exposed cut flesh; not every peel spot or discoloration in a finished dessert.
- Gooding, Bird and Robinson (2001), [Molecular cloning and characterisation of banana fruit polyphenol oxidase](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s004250100553). Public publisher abstract read; Williams cultivar, flesh/peel distinction and pre-existing PPO. No paywall bypass, cultivar generalization or Sweet Escape process inference.
- ACS, [2021 Celebrating Chemistry](https://www.acs.org/content/dam/acsorg/education/outreach/celebrating-chemistry/2021-ncw/2021-ncw-celebrating-chemistry-english.pdf). Relevant enzyme/catalyst and apple-activity pages read. Oxygen and acidity conditions; no shop test, experimental eating directions or unrelated statistics.
- Colorado State Extension, [Drying Fruits](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/drying-fruits/), peer-reviewed, reviewed August 2025. Ascorbic acid is vitamin C and an anti-darkening antioxidant. No drying recipe, dosage, antimicrobial or health claim adopted. Replaces Penn State's guide, whose content was retrievable in search but whose reader-facing URL returned HTTP 403 in a normal browser.
- Nebraska Extension, [How to Prevent Cut Fruit from Turning Brown](https://food.unl.edu/article/how-prevent-cut-fruit-turning-brown/). Includes bananas, cutting near serving, covering/refrigerating, acidic juices and labeled anti-darkening products. No invented recipe, time limit or shelf-life promise.
- WVU Extension, [Freezing Fresh Fruits](https://extension.wvu.edu/files/d/c6ed790c-160e-4679-99f2-3ed63745f5d6/freeze-fresh-fruits-color.pdf). Only the statement that freezing slows enzyme actions is used. No old thawing directions or permanent-enzyme-inactivation claim adopted.
- University of Georgia NCHFP, [Freezing Fruits FAQs](https://nchfp.uga.edu/faqs/fruits/category/faq-freezing). Anti-browning treatment remains relevant when freezing; citric acid/lemon juice less effective than ascorbic acid in that context; slow-freezing ice crystals can damage cell walls. No temperature, duration or recipe copied.
- Sarkar et al. (2024), [Preventing enzymatic browning of freshly cut green bananas](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/fsn3.4284). Original study/abstract and relevant methods: mature green hard fruit under controlled refrigerated conditions, water/lemon/coconut-water treatments versus untreated slices, multiple quality outcomes. Explicitly not a universal ripe-fruit recommendation, storage-life promise or shop recipe.
- FDA, [Selecting and Serving Produce Safely](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/selecting-and-serving-produce-safely). Clean hands/surfaces, running-water washing, raw-meat separation, refrigeration and discarding rotten produce; washing reduces but does not eliminate bacteria. Anti-browning is not a food-safety test.

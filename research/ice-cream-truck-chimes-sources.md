Mechanical principle only, not a claim that the specific patent was installed on a truck. - FHWA [barrier physics](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/Environment/noise/resources/quantifying-benefits-of-abatement-part-1.pdf) on path obstruction and edge diffraction; OpenStax [sound intensity](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/17-3-sound-intensity) on inverse-square idealization and [Doppler effect](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/17-7-the-doppler-effect) for stationary listener and moving source. At 20 mph ≈ 8.94 m/s and with sound speed ≈ 343 m/s, 440×343/(343∓8.94) gives ≈452 Hz approaching and ≈429 Hz receding when motion is directly along line of sight in still air. Illustrative, not measured route coverage, loudness, or advice to approach moving traffic.

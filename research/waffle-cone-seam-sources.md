## Sources checked November 21, 2026

- [Charles E. Menches, US924484A (1909)](https://patents.google.com/patent/US924484A/en): flat pairs of baking plates, decorative grooves, and distinct flange/notch proposal for batter spread and a clean wafer margin.
- [King Arthur Baking, Homemade Waffle Cones](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/homemade-waffle-cones-recipe): freshly baked round rolled over a form and allowed to cool, with a bowl as a separate use. No quantities or copied method. [AU2008235591A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/AU2008235591A1/en): molded versus rolled workflows and the role of sugar in the warm pliability of its wafer. Not a formula or ingredient claim for all cones.
- [Martínez-Navarrete et al. (2004), water sorption in wafers](https://academic.oup.com/ijfst/article/39/5/555/7913289): relationship between moisture and crispness; distinct from warm rolling.
- [Carl Taylor, US1481813A (1924)](https://patents.google.com/patent/US1481813A/en): matched inner and outer tools and rotation timed to follow insertion. [Harry G. Tatosian, US1804039A (1931)](https://patents.google.com/patent/US1804039A/en): baked-wafer pickup, shape held until firm, subsequent stacking and conveying.
- [US3792954A (1974)](https://patents.google.com/patent/US3792954A): prior pointed-tip closure issue, proposed rounded sealed end, consistent nesting and wall clearance. These are that patent's stated aims, not measured leak/strength guarantees.
- [Webster A. Tompkins, US1191485A (1916)](https://patents.google.com/patent/US1191485A/en): final-shape baking in the gap between an inner core and outer split mold. Paper and clay descriptions are clearly non-food geometric analogies.

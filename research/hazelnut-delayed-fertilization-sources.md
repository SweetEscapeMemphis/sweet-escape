# Hazelnut delayed fertilization — sources

Checked November 17, 2026. Canonical: https://www.sweetescapememphis.com/blog/hazelnuts-wait-months-after-pollination.html

- [French National Museum of Natural History](https://www.mnhn.fr/en/common-hazel): male catkins, bud-scale-concealed female flower/red receptive parts, no petals and wind pollination. No claim about local trees or insects never visiting hazel.
- [Oregon State University EM9074](https://extension.oregonstate.edu/catalog/pub/em9074): definitions and undeveloped ovarian tissues at pollination. [Biological Flora: Corylus avellana](https://besjournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/1365-2745.14008): separate flowers, self-incompatibility and timing. General education, not cultivar or planting advice.
- [Liu et al. 2014](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00497-014-0248-9): Corylus heterophylla, tissue sectioning and aniline-blue pollen-tube staining, approximately 26-day tube retardation at bottom of style/cavity. Species/conditions explicit; not a universal length of the whole pollination-to-fertilization interval.
- [Beyhan and Marangoz 2007](https://acikerisim.omu.edu.tr/entities/publication/23201c10-54a9-4122-8bb1-ca84ecdb5436): two-year Samsun work in Tombul/Palaz, 3.5–5 months between pollination and fertilization, May/June fertilization followed by growth to harvest. Not the source of the shop’s nuts.
- [Taghavi et al. 2021](https://www.sciencedirect.com/org/science/article/pii/S0008422021000555): 19 cultivars and five selections, four-year southern Ontario trial, female-first in most sampled cultivars, year effects and contrast with male-first in traditional regions.
- [Hazelnut pollen phenotyping](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7753158/): electrical sensing, viable versus nonviable grain distinction, hydration dependence. No pollen-count/yield guarantee.
- [3D fruit-development imaging](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12491578/): Tonda di Giffoni, sample set December 2022–July 2023, micro-CT reconstruction showed 12 flowers in one bud and differentiated inner growth. Colors are image segmentation, not natural tissue colors.

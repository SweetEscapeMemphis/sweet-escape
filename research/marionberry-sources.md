## Sources checked November 23, 2026

- [USDA National Plant Germplasm System accession PI 553254](https://npgsweb.ars-grin.gov/gringlobal/accessiondetail?id=1448226): George F. Waldo, USDA and Oregon experiment station, Chehalem × Olallie in 1945, selection in 1948, ORUS 928 identifier, release in 1956 and Marion County test/naming history. No exclusivity about where it grows or where a specific scoop’s fruit comes from.
- [Finn & Strik, Pacific Northwest breeding history](https://horticulture.oregonstate.edu/sites/agscid7/files/horticulture/attachments/ah34_finn_strik_blackberry_prod_pnw_113335-43_2016.pdf): regional “berry” naming habit; dates and parentage cross-check. Uses alternate spelling ‘Ollalie’; article follows USDA accession narrative’s ‘Olallie’.
- [Oregon State Extension growing guide](https://extension.oregonstate.edu/catalog/ec-1303-growing-blackberries-your-home-garden) and [cultivar guide, table 1](https://extension.oregonstate.edu/sites/extd8/files/documents/ec1617.pdf): trailing canes and trellising; Marion primarily for a processed market, not exclusively.
- [Finn et al., Fruit Varieties Journal (1997)](https://horticulture.oregonstate.edu/sites/agscid7/files/horticulture/attachments/25_finn_et_al_marion_blackberry_fvj_1997.pdf): delicate drupelet skins, weight/leaking juice, observations on processed color; historical reported traits, not claims about all fruit or Sweet Escape’s formulation.
- [Finn and colleagues, HortScience (2005)](https://www.ars.usda.gov/ARSUserFiles/20721000/SmallFruitBreeding/manuscripts/MarionIQFandpuree.pdf): thornless breeding aim, coded thawed whole berries versus screened purées, scoring scopes, low appearance for small siblings ORUS 1843-1 and -3 versus high overall mean purée ratings, no significant overall purée advantage over Marion. Not a frozen bite test, ice cream test or universal tasting preference.
- [Finn et al., Columbia Star release, HortScience (2014)](https://horticulture.oregonstate.edu/sites/agscid7/files/horticulture/attachments/90_finn_et_al_columbia_star_hs_2014.pdf): replicated Oregon trial berry-weight data and 2011 blind purée evaluation, no significant difference compared with Marion across recorded purée traits in that panel.
- [Heather Arndt Anderson’s OPB galette recipe](https://www.opb.org/article/2023/03/10/superabundant-newsletter-northwest-food-portland-oregon-pi-day-bacon-marion-berry-galette-albino-crab/) and [Oregon Raspberry & Blackberry Commission sandwich recipe](https://oregon-berries.com/recipe/marionberry-cheesecake-ice-cream-sandwiches/): cited only for their original preparations, whole fruit plus cornstarch in pastry versus thawed fruit blended into the frozen base. No official-state-pie, legal or producer claims, recipe replication, or Sweet Escape process inference.

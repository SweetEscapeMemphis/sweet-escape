## Authoritative evidence and scope

1. Smithsonian Lemelson Center, Chris Broda-Bahm, The Straight Truth About the Flexible Drinking Straw (published June 1, 2002): https://invention.si.edu/invention-stories/straight-truth-about-flexible-drinking-straw
   - Read through ordinary browser after text-fetch timeout. Joseph B. Friedman observed daughter Judith struggling with a milkshake through a straight paper straw in his brother's Varsity Sweet Shop, San Francisco, in the 1930s. Screw and dental floss formed corrugations; the removed screw left a bendable region. Historical account, not an exact prototype date or DIY drinking-utensil instruction. Avoided time-sensitive production totals and unsourced folklore.
2. US2094268A, Drinking Tube, Joseph Bernard Friedman, issued September 28, 1937: https://patents.google.com/patent/US2094268A/en
   - Flexible corrugated region near one end above container rim; intended mouthpiece angling without substantially decreasing diameter or blocking passage. Explains purpose and diagram, not Sweet Escape packaging or manufacture.
3. US375962A, Artificial Straw, Marvin Stone, issued January 3, 1888: https://patents.google.com/patent/US375962A/en
   - Actual specification identifies Stone despite Google Patents' incorrect OCR title “District.” Paper strip wound into tube, adhesive edge, waterproof coating such as paraffin; document explicitly references natural straws. Not a first-straw-ever claim, no mint-julep folklore or modern material-performance/recycling claim.
4. Smithsonian NMAH, Joseph B. Friedman Papers, NMAH.AC.0769: https://sova.si.edu/record/nmah.ac.0769
   - Unsuccessful attempts to sell patent, California incorporation 1939, WWII delay, family/associate financing and sister Betty's business advice/sales/distribution, late-1940s first machine, initial hospital market and first sale 1947. No medical-benefit or dietary-safety advice. Collection includes sketches/blueprints/correspondence/company records.
5. US2550797A, Flexible Drinking Straw, Joseph B. Friedman, issued May 1, 1951: https://patents.google.com/patent/US2550797A/en
   - Raised ridges could intermesh during dispensing. Proposed flexible section no wider than end sections and temporary rigidity from a frangible coating for packaging/adjustment. Historical patent proposal, not universal current-straw construction.
6. University of Guelph Physics, AMASE: How Do Straws Work? (April 14, 2020): https://www.physics.uoguelph.ca/news/2020/04/amase-how-do-straws-work
   - Pressure difference: lower pressure inside straw, higher atmospheric pressure on exposed liquid. Smoothie demonstration with different straw arrangements. No universal drinking-time claim, speed challenge, unsafe hardware activity, straw cutting instructions or shop-specific fluid measurement.
7. Original Journal of Fluid Mechanics study, Hydrodynamics of finite-length pipes at intermediate Reynolds numbers: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-fluid-mechanics/article/hydrodynamics-of-finitelength-pipes-at-intermediate-reynolds-numbers/D7BBD977E0A615A3194E664D8752E1CB
   - Straw types tested; ordinary sipping can involve entrance, laminar and turbulent effects together; authors challenge blanket fully-developed-laminar-flow assumption. Not a prediction for Sweet Escape milkshakes or a fabricated viscosity/recipe measurement. No equations or universal performance promise.
